### HIGH — `{#await}` missing `{:catch}` on native playground loader

**File:** `src/routes/[lang]/playgrounds/[slug]/+page.svelte:103–116`

```svelte
{#await nativeLoader}
    <div class="iframe-loading">…spinner…</div>
{:then module}
    {#if module}<module.default … />{/if}
{/await}       ← no {:catch err}
```

If `loadPlaygroundComponent()` rejects (network error, missing chunk, bad default export) the spinner stays visible indefinitely — no error message, no retry affordance. This is a silent failure on the critical render path for all six native playgrounds.

**Fix:** Add `{:catch err}` rendering an error banner with a retry button and a key like `playgrounds.load_error`.

### MEDIUM — Client component imports from `$lib/server/` (server boundary violation)

**File:** `src/lib/components/feedback/FeedbackForm.svelte:7`

```ts
import type { FeedbackType, FeedbackSeverity } from '$lib/server/db/types';
```

`FeedbackForm.svelte` is a client-rendered component. `import type` is erased by TypeScript today, so it doesn't fail — but if `$lib/server/db/types` ever gains a runtime export (enum value, class, Supabase row type), the build will silently pull server code into the client bundle. CLAUDE.md: "Server-only code goes in `$lib/server/` — SvelteKit enforces the boundary."

**Fix:** Extract `FeedbackType` and `FeedbackSeverity` into a shared file such as `src/lib/shared/feedback-types.ts`.

### Idea 1 — Trial Balance & Period Closing
**EN:** Trial Balance & Closing Entries | **FR:** Balance de vérification et clôture d'exercice

**Target:** Licence 1–2

**Pedagogical objective:** Students treat journal entries as isolated events and miss how period-end closing zeroes temporary accounts. The single most common conceptual gap in first-year financial accounting.

**Core interaction:** Post 6–10 guided entries for a SYSCOHADA micro-enterprise. A live two-column trial balance updates with every entry. "Run Closing" generates closing entries for classes 6 and 7, zeroes them into the income summary account, and reveals the post-closing balance.

**MVP inputs/outputs:** Chart-of-accounts picker (reusing `src/lib/shared/chart-of-accounts`), debit/credit amounts → pre-closing trial balance, closing journal entries, post-closing balance, net income figure.

**Complement:** Extends Journal Entry from single-entry mechanics to the full accounting cycle; prerequisite to financial-statement construction.

---

### Idea 2 — Cash Flow Statement Builder
**EN:** Cash Flow Statement | **FR:** Tableau des flux de trésorerie (TFT)

**Target:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Students confuse operating/investing/financing classifications and can't reconcile the TFT's closing cash balance to the balance sheet. SYSCOHADA's indirect method format differs subtly from IFRS and French PCG.

**Core interaction:** Pre-populated balance-sheet changes and income-statement items. Learner drags each into the correct TFT section; incorrect placements are flagged. Three-section total reconciles to the net change in cash.

**MVP inputs/outputs:** ~12 pre-set line items, SYSCOHADA/IFRS toggle → completed TFT, pass/fail per section, final reconciliation check.

**Complement:** Sits downstream of Journal Entry and Bank Reconciliation; consumes their outputs.

---

### Idea 3 — Payroll & Social Charges (OHADA zone)
**EN:** Payroll & Social Charges | **FR:** Paie et charges sociales SYSCOHADA

**Target:** Licence 2–3, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone payroll (CNPS, IRPP, AGA) interactions are opaque; students routinely miscalculate net salary or misclassify the payroll journal entry.

**Core interaction:** Enter gross salary, allowances, dependants, and country (Cameroon/Côte d'Ivoire/Senegal). A stepped panel reveals each deduction. Output: payslip preview + SYSCOHADA class-6 journal entry.

**MVP inputs/outputs:** Gross salary, allowances, country selector, dependants → gross-to-net waterfall, employer cost, class 641/645 journal entry, CNPS/IRPP amounts with regulatory references.

**Complement:** Fills a domain gap absent from every existing playground; key differentiator for the practicing-professional segment.

---

### Idea 4 — VAT Return Simulator
**EN:** VAT Return | **FR:** Déclaration de TVA

**Target:** Licence 2, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Students fail at netting input vs. output VAT, handling credit carry-forwards, and producing the OHADA-zone declaration format (18–19.25 % TVA depending on jurisdiction).

**Core interaction:** Enter purchase and sales invoices for one month. Playground computes net VAT due or credit. Second-month panel shows carry-forward logic.

**MVP inputs/outputs:** 3–5 purchase invoices, 3–5 sales invoices, prior credit brought forward → VAT recap table, net payable/credit, declaration preview, journal entry.

**Complement:** Chains multiple Journal Entry transactions into a compliance output; introduces regulatory time-boxing.

---

### Idea 5 — Financial Ratios Analyser
**EN:** Financial Ratios | **FR:** Analyse financière par ratios

**Target:** Licence 2–3, Master

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can compute individual ratios but can't interpret a ratio profile holistically or map SYSCOHADA's balance-sheet format (permanent vs. circulating capital) to standard ratio formulas.

**Core interaction:** Input a simplified balance sheet + income statement. Eight ratios compute live in four groups (liquidity, solvency, profitability, efficiency). Each ratio card shows formula, value, sector benchmark band, and a plain-language interpretation.

**MVP inputs/outputs:** Total assets, current assets, stocks, receivables, current liabilities, equity, revenue, operating profit, net profit → ratio dashboard with red/amber/green indicators, written interpretation per ratio.

**Complement:** Natural capstone — consumes the financial-statement outputs every other playground helps produce.
