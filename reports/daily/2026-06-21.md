### Issue 4 — `$effect` used to initialize `$state` from a prop (GoalSeekPanel)
**Severity:** medium
**File:** `src/lib/playgrounds/cvp/components/GoalSeekPanel.svelte:43–46`

```svelte
$effect(() => {
  if (targetProfitInput === '') {
    targetProfitInput = fmtNumber(targetProfit || 20000, 0, locale);
  }
});
```

This effect runs once (when `targetProfitInput` is empty string on mount), then never re-runs because the condition is never truthy again. If the parent changes `targetProfit` after mount (e.g., the user loads a new scenario), the input does not update. Additionally, using `$effect` for one-time initialization is an anti-pattern in Svelte 5 — it creates a reactive subscription for no ongoing benefit.

**Suggested fix:** Initialize the state directly at declaration time using the prop:
```svelte
let targetProfitInput = $state(fmtNumber(targetProfit || 20000, 0, locale));
```
If the input must reset when `targetProfit` changes from the parent, use `$derived` with a reset gate or expose a `reset()` function the parent can call.

### Issue 5 — Three redundant `$effect` calls each syncing one tween (VarianceScale)
**Severity:** low

### Idea 1 — Financial Statement Builder (Constructeur d'états financiers)

**Title (EN):** Financial Statement Builder
**Title (FR):** Constructeur d'états financiers SYSCOHADA

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can post journal entries and calculate account balances but struggle to assemble the SYSCOHADA Bilan (balance sheet) and Compte de résultat from a trial balance. This playground bridges the gap between the chart of accounts and published statements.

**Core interaction:** The learner receives a pre-populated trial balance of 12–18 accounts. They drag-and-drop rows into the correct line item of either the Bilan or the Compte de résultat, then click "Calculate" to see whether Assets = Liabilities + Equity and Net Income matches the bottom line. Wrong placements are highlighted with the SYSCOHADA classification number that should have been used.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: trial balance rows (account code, debit balance, credit balance)
- Output: completed Bilan (Actif / Passif) and Compte de résultat, delta check, score
- Difficulty levels: fondamental (7 accounts, all asset/liability), avance (18 accounts including provisions, deferred tax)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry teaches debits/credits; Amortization teaches periodic expense recognition; this closes the loop by teaching how those entries aggregate into SYSCOHADA statutory reports — the final deliverable every accountant must produce.

### Idea 2 — VAT & Tax Entry Simulator (Simulateur TVA / taxes)

**Title (EN):** VAT & Withholding Tax Simulator
**Title (FR):** Simulateur TVA et retenues à la source

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone countries impose varying VAT rates (18–20%) and withholding tax regimes. Students routinely mis-post the recoverable VAT account (4451) vs. collected VAT (4452) and confuse the net/gross/tax triangle. This playground builds automatic fluency.

**Core interaction:** The learner is given a purchase or sale invoice (amount HT, VAT rate, withholding rate if applicable). They must: (1) compute VAT and net amounts, (2) select the correct SYSCOHADA accounts for each line of the journal entry, (3) verify the tax payable balance. A live "tax triangle" diagram updates as they type, showing HT → TVA → TTC relationships.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: invoice amount (HT), VAT rate selector (0%, 10%, 18%, 20%), transaction type (purchase / sale), withholding toggle
- Output: correct journal entry, tax payable/recoverable balances, score
- Bonus: country-specific presets (Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal, Cameroun, Congo)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry teaches the mechanics of posting but does not model tax accounts specifically. This fills an important OHADA-specific gap not addressed by any current playground.

### Idea 3 — Payroll & Social Charges Calculator (Calculateur de paie)

**Title (EN):** Payroll & Social Charges Calculator
**Title (FR):** Calculateur de paie et charges sociales

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / practicing professional (HR/accounting staff)

**Pedagogical objective:** African payroll computation is a frequent source of errors: CNPS contributions, income tax (IRPP) brackets, and the employer/employee split differ by country and are often misapplied. Students learn the computation chain and the resulting accounting entries under SYSCOHADA.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters gross salary and selects a country (Cameroun, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal, Gabon as a starting set). The playground computes: gross → employee deductions (CNPS, IRPP) → net pay; and employer side: CNPS employer share, FDFP training levy. Output includes: net pay slip, employer cost summary, and the full payroll journal entry with SYSCOHADA accounts (641x employee costs, 431x social liabilities, 444x tax payable).

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: gross salary, country selector, number of dependents (for IRPP bracket)
- Output: net pay, deduction breakdown, employer cost, journal entry
- Difficulty: fondamental (one employee, flat rate), avance (multiple employees, month-end accruals)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground touches human resources accounting or tax administration. Payroll is an extremely high-frequency task for OHADA-zone accountants and an area where errors have direct legal exposure.

### Idea 4 — Foreign Currency Transaction Trainer (Transactions en devises)

**Title (EN):** Foreign Currency Transaction Trainer
**Title (FR):** Transactions en devises étrangères

**Target learner:** Master 1 / Master 2 / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA requires revaluation of monetary items at closing rate and recognition of unrealized exchange differences in account 476/477. Students consistently confuse transaction-date recording, payment-date settlement, and year-end revaluation — three separate events each requiring a different entry.

**Core interaction:** The learner steps through a three-stage timeline: (1) invoice date — record the sale/purchase at the spot rate; (2) payment date — record settlement and the realized exchange gain/loss (accounts 776/676); (3) year-end — revalue any open receivable/payable at the closing rate and post the unrealized difference to 476/477. A currency rate input slider makes it easy to see how a weakening or strengthening CFA franc changes each entry.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: invoice amount (foreign currency), invoice date rate, payment date rate, year-end rate, transaction type
- Output: journal entries for all three events, running receivable/payable balance, net FX impact
- Standards: SYSCOHADA primary; IFRS IAS 21 comparison mode for Master students

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The Interest and TVM playgrounds cover time value in a single currency. This is the only multi-currency mechanics topic in the curriculum and directly addresses a Master-level exam question type.

### Idea 5 — Cash Flow Statement Builder (Tableau des flux de trésorerie)

**Title (EN):** Cash Flow Statement Builder (Indirect Method)
**Title (FR):** Tableau des flux de trésorerie — méthode indirecte

**Target learner:** Master 1 / Master 2

**Pedagogical objective:** Most students can produce a P&L but cannot derive the operating cash flow section of the TFT using the indirect method — i.e., adjusting net income for non-cash items and working capital changes. This is a standard DSCG (French CPA) and OHADA audit exam topic.

**Core interaction:** The learner is given pre-populated net income, depreciation charge, and balance sheet deltas (receivables, payables, inventory). They classify each item as Add-back, Deduction, or Working Capital Change, dragging it to the correct row of the TFT template. The running cash-from-operations total updates live. A toggle switches between SYSCOHADA TFT format and IAS 7 for comparison.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: net income, depreciation, change in receivables/payables/inventory, capex
- Output: completed indirect-method TFT (Operating, Investing, Financing sections), net change in cash, score
- Difficulty: fondamental (operating section only), avance (all three sections + financing activities)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The Financial Statement Builder (Idea 1) covers Bilan and Compte de résultat. This completes the trio of SYSCOHADA primary financial statements and introduces the indirect-method reconciliation skill that no current playground addresses.
