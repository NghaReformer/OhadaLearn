### B4 — `requestFullscreen()` promise is neither awaited nor catch-guarded
**Severity:** Medium
**File:** `src/routes/[lang]/playgrounds/[slug]/+page.svelte:46–48`

```ts
target.requestFullscreen();   // Promise<void>, unawaited
isFullscreen = true;          // set synchronously, before promise settles
```

If the browser rejects the request (permissions policy, sandboxed iframe context, or Safari on some versions), the unhandled rejection fires and `isFullscreen` is stuck at `true`, showing an incorrect exit-fullscreen button indefinitely.

**Suggested fix:**
```ts
target.requestFullscreen().then(() => { isFullscreen = true; }).catch(() => {});
```
Or with `async`:
```ts
try { await target.requestFullscreen(); isFullscreen = true; } catch { /* ignored */ }
```

### P1 — Inventory Valuation (FIFO / CUMP / CMUP)
**Title (EN):** Inventory Valuation Methods
**Title (FR):** Méthodes de valorisation des stocks

**Target learner:** Licence 2 (second-year undergraduates)

**Pedagogical objective:** Students consistently confuse "coût moyen unitaire pondéré en fin de période" (CMUP) with "coût moyen unitaire pondéré à l'avancement" (CUMP), and incorrectly treat FIFO as the default under SYSCOHADA. This playground closes the gap by making the choice of method immediately visible in the income statement impact.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a series of purchases (date, quantity, unit cost) and sales events. Three live columns — FIFO, CUMP, CMUP — compute the ending stock value, COGS, and gross profit side-by-side in real time. Clicking a row in the schedule highlights the journal entry (Classe 3 and Classe 6) for that movement.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: opening stock (qty, unit cost), up to 10 purchase rows, up to 10 sale rows
- Outputs: inventory schedule per method, ending stock value per method, COGS per method, delta P&L comparison strip

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Depreciation and Amortization handle asset valuation flows; this handles current-asset flows. No existing playground touches Class 3 (Stocks) accounts. Pairs naturally with Journal Entry to show the SYSCOHADA account codes (311x, 601x).

### P2 — Financial Diagnostic (BFR / FR / TN + Ratio Suite)
**Title (EN):** Financial Diagnostic Dashboard
**Title (FR):** Diagnostic financier — BFR, FR et ratios

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA exams test the "bilan fonctionnel" decomposition (Ressources stables, Emplois stables, BFR d'exploitation, BFR hors exploitation, Trésorerie nette). Students memorise formulas but cannot reason from a balance sheet to a diagnosis. This playground teaches the diagnostic reasoning, not the arithmetic.

Ratios update live: Fonds de Roulement, Besoin en Fonds de Roulement, Trésorerie Nette, current ratio, quick ratio, debt ratio, and rotation days (stock, customers, suppliers). A traffic-light panel flags concerning values with a one-sentence interpretation.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: balance sheet values grouped into SYSCOHADA functional masses (Actif immobilisé, Actif circulant d'exploitation, Dettes financières, etc.)
- Outputs: FR / BFR / TN waterfall, 8-ratio table with benchmarks, written interpretation snippet

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP covers management accounting; this covers financial accounting diagnosis — the other half of a typical L3 exam. No existing playground touches the SYSCOHADA bilan fonctionnel.

### P3 — VAT / TVA Journal Accounting
**Title (EN):** VAT Accounting & Recovery
**Title (FR):** Comptabilisation de la TVA (déductibilité et prorata)

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** VAT accounting in the OHADA zone mixes multiple regimes (régime réel normal, simplifié, franchise). Students misclassify TVA sur achats as an expense instead of a recoverable asset, and almost universally cannot compute the prorata de déduction for mixed-use entities. This playground makes the recoverable/non-recoverable split tangible.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a month of purchase and sales invoices with TVA rates (18% standard in Cameroon, 0% for exports, exempt items). The playground computes TVA collectée, TVA déductible, the net balance due / refundable, and builds the journal entries (Class 4452, 4456, 4457, 4458). A second mode exposes the prorata slider for mixed-use businesses and shows how partial deductibility changes the expense vs asset split.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: sales invoices (HT amount, TVA rate), purchase invoices (HT amount, TVA rate, deductibility %), optional prorata %
- Outputs: TVA déclaration summary table, jounal entries for the period, net position (dû / crédit de TVA)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry covers the mechanics of debit/credit; TVA Accounting applies it to the most exam-tested real-world scenario. No current playground touches Class 44 (État et Collectivités) or mixed-use prorata.

### P4 — Payroll Accounting (Bulletin de paie → Journal)
**Title (EN):** Payroll Journal Entry Builder
**Title (FR):** Comptabilisation de la paie SYSCOHADA

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1 / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Payroll in SYSCOHADA involves employee social charges (CNPS, IPP), employer contributions, and the net-to-gross reconciliation. Students routinely omit employer charges from the journal entry and confuse "charges salariales" (Class 66) with "charges patronales" (also Class 66 but different accounts). The cross-check that gross wage = net wage + salariales + retenue fiscale is consistently missed.

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs gross salary, applicable IPP bracket (OHADA zone reference scale), CNPS employee/employer rates, and CRTV contribution. The playground computes net salary, builds the full payroll journal entry (6611, 6617, 6641, 4411, 4301, 4311, 5211), and highlights each line's Class-6 vs Class-4 vs Class-5 nature. A verification panel confirms debits = credits.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: gross salary, IPP rate (selectable bracket), CNPS employee %, CNPS employer %, other deductions
- Outputs: payslip summary, journal entry with SYSCOHADA account codes, T-account view

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry teaches structure; Payroll teaches a specific high-exam-frequency transaction type with mandatory employer/employee split logic. No current playground covers Class 661x–664x payroll accounts.

### P5 — Provisions for Depreciation of Receivables (Créances douteuses)
**Title (EN):** Provisions for Doubtful Debts
**Title (FR):** Dépréciations de créances — comptabilisation et reprise

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** Candidates confuse "provision pour dépréciation de créance" (balance-sheet impairment, SYSCOHADA Class 416/491) with outright bad-debt write-off. They also lose marks by forgetting the TVA adjustment on write-off (TVA sur créances irrécouvrables — reclaiming previously collected TVA). This playground walks through the full three-year lifecycle of a doubtful debt.

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs an original invoice (HT + TVA), then advances through an interactive timeline: (1) classification as doubtful at year-end with a % probability of loss, (2) year-end provision entry (491x), (3) possible partial recovery or confirmed write-off (654x, reversal of provision), (4) TVA reclaim entry. The balance sheet and P&L strips update at each stage.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: invoice HT, TVA rate, year-1 impairment %, year-2 outcome (recovered / written off / % changed)
- Outputs: journal entries at each stage, Class 41 customer account ledger extract, P&L impact per year, TVA reclaim calculation

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Bank Reconciliation handles timing differences in cash; this handles credit risk in the SYSCOHADA receivables lifecycle. No existing playground covers Class 49 (Dépréciations des comptes de tiers) or the TVA récupérable on bad debt.
