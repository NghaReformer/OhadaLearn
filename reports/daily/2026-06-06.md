### Issue detail

#### [1] `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/CategoryBreakdownDonut.svelte:31–40`
**Severity:** High  
**Problem:** A `CATEGORY_COLORS` constant holds 9 raw hex values (`#6ea8fe`, `#22c55e`, `#f59e0b`, `#10b981`, `#ef4444`, `#a855f7`, `#06b6d4`, `#ec4899`, `#fb923c`). These are not part of the design token system and will not respect future theme changes.  
**Fix:** Add a `--chart-*` palette (9 slots) to `src/lib/theme/tokens.ts` and emit corresponding CSS custom properties via `css-generator.ts`. Replace the constant with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).getPropertyValue('--chart-1')` … `--chart-9`, read once in an `$effect`.

### Playground A — Balance Sheet Builder
**EN:** Balance Sheet Builder · **FR:** Constructeur de bilan comptable  
**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3  
**Pedagogical objective:** Many students can classify individual accounts but struggle to assemble a SYSCOHADA-compliant bilan in the correct order (immobilisations → stocks → créances → trésorerie on assets; capitaux propres → dettes financières → dettes à court terme on liabilities). This playground fixes the assembly skill gap.  
**Core interaction:** A drag-and-drop interface where learners place ~20 pre-defined account balances into the correct section and sub-section of the two-column bilan. The playground validates classification, ordering, and that total assets = total liabilities + equity in real time.  
**Minimum viable inputs:** 20 account cards (name, amount, debit/credit), learner drag placement.  
**Minimum viable outputs:** Live bilan preview with section totals, colour-coded correct/incorrect placements, a final balance-check indicator.  
**Complement:** Journal-entry playground produces individual transactions; this playground teaches what those entries aggregate into. Natural sequel in the curriculum.

### Playground B — VAT Filing Simulator
**EN:** VAT Filing Simulator · **FR:** Simulateur de déclaration TVA  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master / practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone VAT (18 % standard, exempt categories, pro-rata for mixed taxpayers) is error-prone because learners conflate collectée, déductible, and net à payer. This playground makes the three-bucket structure visceral.  
**Core interaction:** Learner enters a list of sales transactions (amount, VAT rate) and purchase transactions (amount, VAT rate, deductibility flag). The playground auto-computes TVA collectée, TVA déductible, pro-rata coefficient for mixed inputs, and net à décaisser / crédit à reporter. Learner can toggle between monthly and quarterly filing periods.  
**Minimum viable inputs:** N sales rows (HT amount, taux), N purchase rows (HT amount, taux, fully/partially/non deductible flag), filing period toggle.  
**Minimum viable outputs:** Three-bucket summary (collectée / déductible / solde), filled mock declaration form, running credit carry-forward tracker.  
**Complement:** CVP playground handles cost structure; this handles the tax layer on top — different conceptual domain.

### Playground C — Financial Ratio Analyser
**EN:** Financial Ratio Analyser · **FR:** Analyseur de ratios financiers  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students memorise ratio formulas but rarely build the intuition for what moves a ratio and whether a value is healthy. This playground teaches interpretation, not just calculation.  
**Core interaction:** Learner inputs a simplified SYSCOHADA trial balance (or selects a pre-built sector scenario: commerce, BTP, agriculture). The playground computes ~12 ratios grouped by category (liquidité, solvabilité, rentabilité, gestion). For each ratio, a visual gauge shows the OHADA sector benchmark range. Learner can nudge individual account balances with a slider and see which ratios respond — building sensitivity intuition.  
**Minimum viable inputs:** 8–10 balance-sheet line items, 4–6 income-statement line items, sector selector.  
**Minimum viable outputs:** Ratio dashboard with gauge visualisations, benchmark overlays, change-tracking arrows when inputs are edited.  
**Complement:** Bank-reconciliation playground operates at the transaction level; this operates at the aggregate reporting level — complementary abstraction layer.

### Playground D — Cash Flow Statement Builder (Indirect Method)
**EN:** Cash Flow Statement Builder · **FR:** Constructeur du tableau des flux de trésorerie  
**Target learner:** Master / practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** The indirect method cash flow statement is the most frequently misunderstood SYSCOHADA statement — students confuse net income adjustments (adding back depreciation, removing working-capital changes) with actual cash movements. This playground makes every adjustment visible.  
**Core interaction:** Learner is given a comparative balance sheet and an income statement. They drag adjustment items into one of three sections (exploitation, investissement, financement). The playground validates each placement and shows a running "cash explained" tracker that must reach the period's net change in cash. Tooltips explain each SYSCOHADA-specific classification rule.  
**Minimum viable inputs:** 12-line comparative balance sheet, 8-line income statement (all pre-filled, read-only). Learner actions: classification drag-and-drop only.  
**Minimum viable outputs:** Three-section cash flow statement auto-assembled from learner classifications, final cash reconciliation (opening + net = closing), colour-coded correctness feedback.  
**Complement:** The amortization playground covers one specific adjustment (depreciation add-back); this playground contextualises it within the full statement.

### Playground E — Break-Even & Margin of Safety (Multi-Currency)
**EN:** Margin of Safety Explorer · **FR:** Explorateur de marge de sécurité  
**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3  
**Pedagogical objective:** The existing CVP playground covers break-even at the product level. This playground focuses on the *margin of safety* concept (actual sales − break-even sales) and its operational leverage implications — a distinct and commonly tested SYSCOHADA exam topic.  
**Core interaction:** Learner sets fixed costs, variable cost %, and a target sales figure. The playground draws a cost-volume-profit chart with three zones labelled (perte, seuil de rentabilité, marge de sécurité). Learner adjusts any variable with a slider; the chart and key metrics update live. A "stress test" button simulates a 10 %, 20 %, 30 % demand drop and shows whether the margin of safety absorbs it.  
**Minimum viable inputs:** Fixed costs, variable cost ratio, actual/target revenue. Currency selector (FCFA, EUR, USD via the existing format system).  
**Minimum viable outputs:** CVP chart with zone shading, margin of safety in absolute and percentage terms, operating leverage factor, stress-test results panel.  
**Complement:** CVP playground centres on the break-even calculation itself; this one centres on *what happens after* break-even — a natural next step in the curriculum sequence.
