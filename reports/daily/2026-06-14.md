### BUG-001 — Missing `--orange` theme token (hardcoded fallback always active)

**Severity:** High
**File:** `src/lib/components/playground/KpiStrip.svelte:76`

```css
color: var(--orange, #f59e0b);
```

`--orange` is not defined in `src/lib/theme/tokens.ts`, so the CSS variable never resolves and the hardcoded fallback `#f59e0b` is always used. This silently bypasses the token system — if the design theme changes, this KPI color will not follow.

**Suggested fix:** Add `orange: '#f59e0b'` to `defaultTheme.colors` in `src/lib/theme/tokens.ts` and ensure `css-generator.ts` emits `--orange`. Once the token is defined, the fallback becomes a true safety net rather than the only path.

### IDEA-01 — Trial Balance & Period Closing

**Title (EN):** Trial Balance & Closing Entries
**Title (FR):** Balance de Vérification & Écritures de Clôture

**Target learner:** Licence 1–2 (core financial-accounting course)

**Pedagogical objective:** Students confuse the trial balance with the final balance sheet and often do not understand which accounts are "temporary" (income/expense → Classe 6/7 under SYSCOHADA) versus "permanent" (assets/liabilities → Classe 1–5). This playground forces them to correctly close P&L accounts to Résultat de l'exercice (account 13x).

**Core interaction:** Student receives a pre-populated list of ledger balances (10–15 accounts). They must: (1) verify the trial balance totals, (2) identify which accounts to close, (3) enter the closing journal entries, and (4) see the resulting balance sheet stub. The playground validates debit/credit symmetry at each step and marks each closed account.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: Pre-randomized account list from SYSCOHADA Classe 1–7 with debit/credit balances
- Outputs: Trial balance totals, pass/fail on closing entries, post-closing retained-earnings figure

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The Journal Entry playground teaches individual entries; this playground teaches the full period-end workflow — the natural next step after mastering debits and credits.

---

### IDEA-02 — SYSCOHADA Financial Statement Builder

**Title (EN):** Financial Statement Builder (SYSCOHADA)
**Title (FR):** Constructeur d'États Financiers (SYSCOHADA)

**Target learner:** Licence 2–3, practicing professionals onboarding to SYSCOHADA from IFRS or French PCG

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA's balance sheet structure (Actif immobilisé / Actif circulant / Trésorerie Actif vs. Capitaux propres / Dettes financières / Passif circulant / Trésorerie Passif) differs from French PCG and IFRS. Students misclassify accounts or use wrong subtotals. The playground makes classification errors immediately visible.

**Core interaction:** Given a list of 20+ trial balance accounts, the student drags each into the correct cell of the SYSCOHADA Bilan and Compte de Résultat. The playground computes running subtotals in real time (Ressources Stables, Actif Net, Résultat net) and highlights structural mismatches. A "check" button reveals the correct classification with explanations.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: Drag-and-drop account list; selectable difficulty (Licence 1 = 10 accounts, Licence 3 = 25+ with provisions and deferred items)
- Outputs: Completed Bilan and Compte de Résultat with automatic totalling; score on first/second/third attempt

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground covers the SYSCOHADA statement structure. This is the capstone that unifies the Journal Entry and Trial Balance playgrounds into a full accounting cycle.

---

### IDEA-03 — Inventory Valuation (CMUP / FIFO / LIFO)

**Title (EN):** Inventory Valuation Methods
**Title (FR):** Méthodes de Valorisation des Stocks

**Target learner:** Licence 2, managerial-accounting track

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA mandates CMUP (Coût Moyen Unitaire Pondéré after each movement or at period-end) and permits FIFO; LIFO is forbidden under IFRS. Students often apply CMUP formula incorrectly — particularly the "weighted average after each issue" variant versus the "periodic" variant. The side-by-side comparison makes the numeric difference concrete.

**Core interaction:** Student enters a sequence of inventory movements (purchases with unit cost, sales). The playground computes stock valuations under all three methods in parallel columns (CMUP periodic, CMUP perpetual, FIFO). Each movement updates the running stock card in real time. A final panel shows CoGS and ending inventory for each method and the delta.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: Opening balance (qty, unit cost); up to 10 purchase/sale movements; selectable method to "submit as answer"
- Outputs: Per-movement stock card (qty, unit cost, total value); summary table comparing CoGS and closing stock across methods; P&L impact delta

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The Amortization playground covers long-term asset costs; this fills the short-term asset (current asset) gap — a separate SYSCOHADA exam topic.

### IDEA-04 — Payroll & Social Charges Calculator (OHADA Zone)

**Title (EN):** Payroll & Social Charges (CNPS)
**Title (FR):** Bulletin de Paie & Charges Sociales (CNPS)

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1; HR/payroll professionals

**Pedagogical objective:** CNPS contribution rates (OHADA zone social security) and income-tax withholding (IRPP) are frequently miscalculated, especially the employer-vs-employee split and the treatment of in-kind benefits. Most students have never produced a compliant payslip. This playground eliminates the mystery of "how does gross become net."

**Core interaction:** Student inputs gross salary, optional bonuses, and in-kind benefits. The playground computes the full payslip breakdown: employee CNPS deductions, IRPP withholding (progressive brackets), net pay, employer CNPS charges, total employer cost. A toggle lets students switch between country-level CNPS rate schedules (Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal, Bénin) to compare. The corresponding SYSCOHADA journal entries (comptes 641, 645, 431, 447) are generated automatically.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: Gross salary, bonus, in-kind benefits value, country selector, pay period
- Outputs: Payslip table (gross → deductions → net); employer charge breakdown; total monthly employment cost; journal entry preview

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground touches payroll. Gross→net calculation is a mandatory Licence 3 and professional competency that directly generates SYSCOHADA class-6 journal entries — a natural bridge to the Journal Entry playground.

---

### IDEA-05 — Financial Ratio Analysis Dashboard

**Title (EN):** Financial Ratio Analysis
**Title (FR):** Analyse par les Ratios Financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1 / practicing professionals

**Pedagogical objective:** Students memorize ratio formulas in isolation but cannot interpret them in context or diagnose a firm's financial health from a set of ratios. This playground teaches ratio families (liquidity, solvency, profitability, activity) and their SYSCOHADA-specific numerators/denominators (e.g., FR = Ressources Durables − Emplois Stables).

**Core interaction:** Student receives simplified SYSCOHADA Bilan and Compte de Résultat figures (editable). The playground computes 10–12 key ratios in real time, plotted as gauge charts with colour-coded zones (healthy / watch / danger). A "compare to sector" overlay shows average values for a selectable industry (Trade, Manufacturing, Services). Students can tweak individual line items and watch ratios respond, revealing which levers have the largest effect.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: Balance sheet line items (Actif immobilisé, Stocks, Créances, Trésorerie, Capitaux propres, Dettes financières, Dettes CT); Income statement items (CA, Charges d'exploitation, Résultat net); sector selector
- Outputs: Ratios: liquidité générale, liquidité réduite, solvabilité, autonomie financière, rentabilité des capitaux propres (ROE), rentabilité des actifs (ROA), rotation des stocks, délai de recouvrement; gauge visualisations; narrative interpretation per ratio

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The CVP playground analyses profitability from a cost-structure perspective; this playground analyses it from a financial-position perspective — together they cover the full managerial and financial accounting lens expected in SYSCOHADA professional exams.
