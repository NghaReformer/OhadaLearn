#### M-2 — Unhandled rejection in `loadLearnSections`
**File:** `src/lib/content/loader.ts:61`
`await renderMarkdown(source)` calls `unified().process()` which can throw on malformed markdown. The call is inside a `for` loop with no try/catch; a single bad markdown file will crash the whole server-side `+page.server.ts` route without a meaningful error message.
**Fix:** Wrap the `renderMarkdown` call in `try { html = await renderMarkdown(source) } catch (e) { html = ''; console.error('markdown parse error:', file, e) }`.

#### M-3 — Three redundant `$effect` blocks driving tweens in `VarianceScale.svelte`
**File:** `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/VarianceScale.svelte:54–62`
Three consecutive one-liner `$effect` blocks each call `.set()` on a single tween. Svelte 5 schedules each effect independently, creating three microtask queue entries on every change. A single `$effect` block that calls all three `.set()` calls is equivalent and registers only one subscriber.
**Fix:** Consolidate into `$effect(() => { tiltTween.set(tiltTarget); bankTween.set(adjustedBank); booksTween.set(adjustedBooks); });`

### Idea 1 — Inventory Valuation Methods

**EN:** Inventory Valuation Methods — FIFO, LIFO, CUMP
**FR:** Méthodes d'évaluation des stocks — FIFO, LIFO, CUMP

**Target learner:** Licence 2 (first cost accounting course)

**Pedagogical objective:** Students routinely conflate FIFO, LIFO, and weighted average cost (Coût Unitaire Moyen Pondéré — the SYSCOHADA-preferred method). They calculate closing stock value correctly for CUMP but make errors when purchase prices change mid-period.

**Core interaction:** The learner enters a sequence of stock movements (purchases at varying unit costs, then sales). The playground displays a running stock card (fiche de stock) in SYSCOHADA columnar format and computes closing stock value and COGS under each of the three methods simultaneously, allowing direct comparison.

**Minimum viable inputs:** initial balance (qty + unit cost), up to 8 purchase rows (date, qty, unit cost), up to 8 sale rows (date, qty). **Outputs:** stock card per method, closing stock value, COGS, gross margin comparison table.

**Complement:** The journal-entry playground records what happened; this one explains at what cost. Together they cover both the recognition and measurement sides of inventory.

### Idea 2 — VAT / TVA Déclaration

**EN:** VAT Return Builder
**FR:** Constructeur de déclaration de TVA

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master 1, practicing bookkeepers

**Pedagogical objective:** TVA in OHADA jurisdictions (CEMAC, UEMOA) has multiple rates and a pro-rata deduction mechanism for mixed-use entities. Students confuse TVA collectée, TVA déductible, and the TVA net à payer / crédit de TVA outcome — especially when some purchases are exempt.

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs sales lines (amount HT, TVA rate: 0%, 9.5% or 18% depending on jurisdiction setting), then purchase lines with their deductibility status. The playground calculates the monthly TVA return form in real time, highlighting the net position and whether a refund claim is triggered.

**Minimum viable inputs:** jurisdiction selector (CEMAC / UEMOA), up to 10 sales lines (description, amount HT, rate), up to 10 purchase lines (description, amount HT, rate, deductible: yes/partial/no). **Outputs:** TVA return table (collectée, déductible, crédit/net), journal entry stub for the TVA account settlement.

**Complement:** Fills a critical curriculum gap not touched by any existing playground. Leverages the `fmtCurrency` formatter and the jurisdiction concept already modelled in `stores/preferences.ts`.

### Idea 3 — Payroll Calculator (Bulletin de Paie)

**EN:** Payroll Slip Builder
**FR:** Simulateur de bulletin de paie

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, HR / accounting professionals

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone payroll involves layered social contributions (CNPS employer + employee shares, CNRPS for civil servants, professional tax, housing levy) that many graduates cannot compute from first principles. The interaction makes the deduction waterfall visible and memorable.

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs gross salary, employment category, country (Cameroun / Sénégal / Côte d'Ivoire — each has different CNPS ceiling and rate). The playground renders a formatted bulletin de paie showing each line: gross, each contribution with its rate and base, net imposable, IRPP withholding (simplified bracket), and net à payer. Sliders let the learner see how a salary change ripples through all deductions.

**Minimum viable inputs:** country selector, gross salary, employment type (permanent / contractual), family status (for IRPP bracket). **Outputs:** formatted bulletin de paie, employer cost total, effective tax rate display.

**Complement:** No existing playground touches labour costs. Directly relevant to management-accounting modules where payroll is a major cost centre.

### Idea 4 — Financial Statement Builder (Bilan + CdR SYSCOHADA)

**EN:** SYSCOHADA Financial Statement Assembler
**FR:** Assembleur d'états financiers SYSCOHADA

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** Students who can correctly post journal entries still struggle to map a trial balance to the official SYSCOHADA balance sheet (classes 1–5) and income statement (classes 6–7) format. The classification step — especially distinguishing current vs. non-current assets and identifying the correct row in the normalized form — is consistently a weak point.

**Core interaction:** Learner is given a randomized list of ~20 trial balance lines (account number + balance). They drag-and-drop each account into its slot on a SYSCOHADA-formatted bilan and compte de résultat. The playground validates placement and computes totals; it flags common errors (e.g., putting a customer advance in non-current liabilities).

**Minimum viable inputs:** pre-seeded trial balance (no manual entry needed — the challenge is classification). **Outputs:** completed bilan with actif / passif balance check, CdR with résultat net, and a score with per-line feedback.

**Complement:** Bridges the gap between the journal-entry playground (individual transactions) and real financial reporting. Uses the same account-code taxonomy already present in `src/routes/[lang]/chart-of-accounts/`.

### Idea 5 — Ratio Analysis Dashboard

**EN:** Financial Ratio Analysis
**FR:** Analyse par les ratios financiers**

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, credit analysts

**Pedagogical objective:** Ratio analysis is taught abstractly in textbooks; students cannot intuitively connect a change in working-capital management to solvency ratios. An interactive dashboard that recalculates 10+ ratios on the fly from editable balance-sheet and income-statement inputs fills this gap.

**Core interaction:** Learner edits a simplified SYSCOHADA bilan + CdR (or selects a built-in industry scenario: trading company, manufacturing, services). The dashboard recomputes ratios in real time — liquidity (ratio de liquidité générale / réduite), leverage (autonomie financière, endettement), profitability (ROE, ROA, marge nette), and activity (rotation des stocks, délai clients/fournisseurs). Radar chart shows the risk profile.

**Minimum viable inputs:** ~15 balance-sheet cells, ~8 income-statement cells, industry benchmark toggle. **Outputs:** ratio table with colour-coded deviation from sector benchmark, radar chart, one-sentence interpretation per ratio in EN/FR.

**Complement:** Extends the CVP playground's analytical approach from cost structure to overall financial health. Reuses the `BreakEvenChart` SVG infrastructure and the `$derived` pattern from the CVP engine for reactive recalculation.
