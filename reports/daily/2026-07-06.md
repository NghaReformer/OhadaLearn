### Idea 1 — Financial Statements Builder
**Title (EN):** Financial Statements Builder  
**Title (FR):** Constructeur des États Financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 2–3 (second/third-year undergrads)

**Pedagogical objective:** Students routinely compute individual account balances but struggle to assemble the three required SYSCOHADA statements (Bilan, Compte de résultat, Tableau de flux de trésorerie) from a trial balance. The misconception is that financial statements are just a data-dump; the playground makes the articulation of statements visible.

**Core interaction:** The learner receives a randomised adjusted trial balance (15–25 accounts drawn from the OHADA chart-of-accounts data already in `src/lib/shared/chart-of-accounts/`). They drag or assign each account to the correct statement and line item. The three statements populate in real time as accounts are placed; running totals flag misclassifications instantly. A "Check" step validates that the income statement net income flows into the balance sheet and that the cash flow statement reconciles.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Inputs: pre-populated trial balance rows (account code, debit/credit balance)  
- Outputs: live Bilan, Compte de résultat, and Tableau de flux TN (indirect method), a pass/fail score, and a journal-entry-style correction if wrong

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The Journal Entry playground builds individual entries; this playground bridges entries → statements, filling the most common gap in the SYSCOHADA curriculum. It reuses `src/lib/shared/chart-of-accounts/` directly and fits the `PlaygroundModule` contract with minimal new infrastructure.

### Idea 2 — Inventory Valuation Methods
**Title (EN):** Inventory Valuation Methods (FIFO, AVCO, LIFO*)  
**Title (FR):** Méthodes de valorisation des stocks (FIFO, CMUP, LIFO*)

**Target learner:** Licence 2, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA mandates FIFO or weighted-average cost (CMUP) — LIFO is forbidden for OHADA-zone enterprises. Students frequently confuse the methods, do not understand why LIFO is excluded, and make arithmetic errors on CMUP recalculation after each entry. The playground surfaces these constraints explicitly.

**Core interaction:** The learner enters a sequence of stock movements (purchases with unit cost, sales, returns) via a simple tabular form. Choosing between FIFO and CMUP recalculates the inventory valuation card and closing stock value in real time. A toggle shows the theoretical LIFO figure with a banner explaining why SYSCOHADA prohibits it and by how much it understates profit.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Inputs: opening stock (qty, unit cost), up to 10 purchase/sale rows, method selector  
- Outputs: stock movement card, closing stock value, COGS, and a delta panel comparing FIFO vs CMUP impact on gross profit

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground touches the stock cycle. The format/currency system handles monetary display; no new numerical infrastructure needed beyond array iteration.

### Idea 3 — Tax Provision & VAT Reconciliation
**Title (EN):** VAT & Tax Provision Calculator  
**Title (FR):** Calculateur de TVA et provision pour impôt

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Students often treat VAT as a simple percentage without understanding input/output VAT netting, the quarterly declaration cycle, or how a tax provision is booked in SYSCOHADA accounts (447 for VAT payable, 444 for tax payable). The playground makes the netting and provision journal entries interactive.

**Core interaction:** The learner enters a month's worth of sales (with output VAT) and purchases (with deductible input VAT). The playground computes net VAT payable or credit, generates the SYSCOHADA accounts 4431/4452 posting, and shows the balance sheet impact. A second tab lets the learner book the corporate-tax provision (account 441) given pre-tax income and a configurable tax rate (OHADA-zone rates default to Cameroon/Côte d'Ivoire/Senegal).

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Inputs: sales rows (HT amount, VAT rate), purchase rows (HT amount, deductible %), corporate tax rate, pre-tax income  
- Outputs: VAT declaration summary, journal entries (using the shared OHADA chart-of-accounts), net tax payable

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** VAT and corporate tax are high-exam-weight topics with no current coverage. Reuses the `AccountPicker` component and journal-entry rendering from the Journal Entry playground.

### Idea 4 — Lease Accounting (IFRS 16 / SYSCOHADA Comparison)
**Title (EN):** Lease Accounting & Amortization (Right-of-Use)  
**Title (FR):** Comptabilisation des contrats de location (droit d'utilisation)

**Target learner:** Master, practicing professional (IFRS adopters in OHADA zone)

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA Révisé 2017 introduced right-of-use asset accounting for finance leases, aligned with IFRS 16. Students and practitioners struggle with the initial measurement (present value of lease payments), the split between finance charge and liability amortisation, and how SYSCOHADA accounts (2813, 162) differ from pure IFRS treatment. The amortization playground covers asset depreciation — this fills the lease-specific gap.

**Core interaction:** The learner inputs lease terms (commencement date, lease term in months, monthly payment, incremental borrowing rate, optional residual). The playground builds the full lease amortisation schedule (identical in structure to the existing amortisation playground), displays the right-of-use asset balance and lease liability on a mini balance sheet, and generates the initial recognition journal entry and each period's finance cost / liability reduction entries in SYSCOHADA codes.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Inputs: lease start date, term (months), payment amount, payment frequency, discount rate, residual value  
- Outputs: amortisation schedule (interest, principal, closing liability), right-of-use depreciation schedule, initial journal entry, period journal entries

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Extends the Amortisation playground's engine (`src/lib/playgrounds/amortization/engine.ts`) with minimal additions — the PV calculation already exists in the TVM playground's solver. The two playgrounds could even cross-link.

### Idea 5 — Ratio Analysis Dashboard
**Title (EN):** Financial Ratio Analysis  
**Title (FR):** Analyse par les ratios financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can compute individual ratios but do not understand how they interact or what thresholds signal distress. SYSCOHADA curricula require ratio analysis using the Bilan condensé (condensed balance sheet) format, which differs from IFRS presentation. The playground makes the ratio landscape navigable and teaches sensitivity.

**Core interaction:** The learner enters simplified balance sheet and income statement figures (7–10 fields each, pre-filled with a realistic OHADA-zone SME scenario). The dashboard instantly renders 15+ SYSCOHADA-relevant ratios grouped by category (liquidity, solvency, profitability, activity) with traffic-light colouring and sector benchmarks (pre-coded for commerce, manufacturing, services). A "What-if" slider lets learners adjust one input and see which ratios cross their thresholds.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Inputs: current assets, fixed assets, current liabilities, long-term debt, equity, revenue, EBITDA, net income  
- Outputs: ratio grid with formulas visible, traffic-light status, what-if sensitivity for the two most impactful inputs

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The CVP playground covers profitability from a cost-structure angle; this covers it from the investor/creditor angle. The format system already handles multi-currency ratios. The `KpiStrip` component in `src/lib/components/playground/KpiStrip.svelte` can be reused directly for the ratio tiles.
