## 1. Bugs & Issues

### HIGH

#### H-1 · Hardcoded hex colors in chart component
**File:** `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/CategoryBreakdownDonut.svelte:30–39`
**Severity:** High
Nine hex literals are hardcoded directly in a TypeScript constant (`CATEGORY_COLORS`), bypassing the theme system entirely. On a dark theme rebrand, these colors will not update.

```ts
'outstanding-check': '#6ea8fe',
'deposit-in-transit': '#22c55e',
// … seven more
```

**Fix:** Add chart-category tokens to `src/lib/theme/tokens.ts` (e.g. `chartColors: { cat1: '#6ea8fe', … }`) and expose them as CSS custom properties (`--chart-cat-outstanding-check` etc.). Reference via `var(--chart-cat-*)` in CSS, not inline TS.

#### M-4 · `color: #fff` used in four files instead of a theme token
**Files:**
- `src/lib/playgrounds/journal-entry/components/EntryHistory.svelte:400`
- `src/routes/[lang]/chart-of-accounts/+page.svelte:423`
- `src/routes/[lang]/chart-of-accounts/+page.svelte:458`
- `src/routes/[lang]/learn/+page.svelte:202`

**Severity:** Medium
All four occurrences are `color: #fff` on text rendered over an `--accent`-colored background. The theme has no `--text-on-accent` token, so the workaround is a bare hex white. If the accent color ever changes to a light hue, text becomes illegible.

**Fix:** Add `textOnAccent: '#ffffff'` to `tokens.ts` → generates `--text-on-accent`. Replace all four instances with `color: var(--text-on-accent)`.

## 2. New Playground Ideas

Existing playgrounds cover: loan amortization, asset depreciation, bank reconciliation, journal entry, CVP/break-even, time value of money, and simple/compound interest. The proposals below fill adjacent SYSCOHADA gaps.

### P-1 · Budget Variance Analysis / Analyse des Écarts Budgétaires
**Title EN:** Budget Variance Analysis | **Title FR:** Analyse des Écarts Budgétaires
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1, practicing professional
**Pedagogical objective:** Students confuse favorable vs. unfavorable variances and cannot decompose a total variance into price and quantity components. This playground makes the decomposition tangible.
**Core interaction:** Learner enters standard (budgeted) unit cost/quantity and actual figures for up to 4 cost lines. The engine computes price variance, efficiency variance, and volume variance in real time; a waterfall chart shows each component's contribution to the total gap.
**Minimum viable inputs:** Standard price, actual price, standard quantity, actual quantity per line. Outputs: individual variances, total variance, favorable/unfavorable flags, SYSCOHADA journal entry for the variance account (class 6 subaccounts).
**Why it fits:** Complements CVP (which is forward-looking) with a backward-looking control analysis. Shares the `NumberField`, `KpiStrip`, and `BalanceIndicator` components. The variance decomposition logic is pure arithmetic — easy to unit-test.

---

### P-2 · TVA / VAT Journal Entry Simulator
**Title EN:** VAT Journal Entry Simulator | **Title FR:** Simulateur d'Écritures TVA
**Target learner:** Licence 1 / Licence 2
**Pedagogical objective:** Most Licence 1 errors involve confusing collected TVA (a liability, account 443) with deductible TVA (an asset, account 4452/4454) and failing to net them on the monthly declaration. This playground makes the dual-entry mechanic visual.
**Core interaction:** Learner selects transaction type (purchase / sale / mixed), enters HT amount and TVA rate. The playground auto-populates a journal entry template with debit/credit sides and the learner must confirm or correct each account code before submission. A running "TVA position" KPI shows net balance owed to/from the tax authority.
**Minimum viable inputs:** Transaction type, HT amount, TVA rate (0%, 5%, 10%, 18% — OHADA-common rates). Outputs: completed journal entry, running TVA account balances, graded feedback per line.
**Why it fits:** Extends the Journal Entry playground with domain-specific scaffolding for a topic that is universally present in OHADA-zone practical exams. Reuses the full journal-entry grading engine (`src/lib/grading/journal-entry.ts`) and `AccountPicker`.

---

### P-3 · Inventory Valuation (FIFO / CMP) / Valorisation des Stocks
**Title EN:** Inventory Valuation Methods | **Title FR:** Méthodes de Valorisation des Stocks
**Target learner:** Licence 2
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA allows FIFO and Coût Moyen Pondéré (CMP). Students apply them mechanically but cannot predict which produces higher COGS in an inflationary environment or how closing stock affects the balance sheet.
**Core interaction:** Learner builds a sequence of stock movements (opening balance, purchases, issues) by dragging/clicking rows in a simple table. The engine computes the stock card in real time under whichever method is selected (FIFO vs CMP toggle). A comparison strip shows the difference in COGS and closing stock value between the two methods.
**Minimum viable inputs:** Up to 10 movement rows (date, type, quantity, unit cost for purchases). Outputs: stock card table with running balance, COGS total, closing inventory value, side-by-side comparison KPIs.
**Why it fits:** Fills a gap between the Journal Entry (records the issue) and no existing valuation playground. Shares `KpiStrip`, `NumberField`, and `fmtCurrency`. The engine is pure functional TS — easy to test with Vitest.

---

### P-4 · Working Capital (BFR) Analyzer / Analyseur du Besoin en Fonds de Roulement
**Title EN:** Working Capital (BFR) Analyzer | **Title FR:** Analyseur du Besoin en Fonds de Roulement
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master
**Pedagogical objective:** "BFR" (Besoin en Fonds de Roulement) is the central concept of SYSCOHADA short-term financial analysis, yet students routinely compute it from the wrong balance sheet lines. This playground builds intuition for how payment terms and inventory days drive cash needs.
**Core interaction:** Learner inputs simplified balance-sheet sections (current assets broken into inventory, receivables; current liabilities broken into payables, tax) and the engine computes BFR, FR (Fonds de Roulement), and TN (Trésorerie Nette) in real time with a three-bar waterfall chart. Sliders for DSO, DIO, DPO show how operational levers shift the BFR.
**Minimum viable inputs:** Inventory, receivables (HT), payables (HT), other current items; revenue/COGS for day-count conversion. Outputs: FR, BFR, TN, days-outstanding metrics, color-coded surplus/deficit indicator.
**Why it fits:** Sits between the static financial analysis concepts taught in Licence 3 and the existing interest/TVM tools. Unique to SYSCOHADA because the French "BFR" terminology and balance-sheet structure differ from IFRS presentations. Uses the `BalanceIndicator` and `KpiStrip` shared components.

### P-5 · Financial Ratios Dashboard / Tableau de Bord des Ratios Financiers
**Title EN:** Financial Ratios Dashboard | **Title FR:** Tableau de Bord des Ratios Financiers
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master / practicing professional
**Pedagogical objective:** Ratio analysis is often taught as a list to memorize. Students cannot interpret contradictory signals (e.g., good liquidity but declining profitability) or benchmark against OHADA-sector norms.
**Core interaction:** Learner inputs 10–15 simplified balance-sheet and income-statement line items. The engine computes 12 ratios grouped by category (liquidity, solvency, profitability, activity) per SYSCOHADA nomenclature. A radar chart overlays the learner's company against a pre-built sector benchmark. Hovering a ratio shows its formula and an explanatory tooltip.
**Minimum viable inputs:** Total assets, current assets, cash, receivables, inventory, current liabilities, total equity, revenue, COGS, EBIT, net income. Outputs: 12 computed ratios, radar chart, textual interpretation per ratio (green/amber/red signal).
**Why it fits:** Complements all existing quantitative playgrounds by adding the diagnostic layer — "what does this number mean?" Serves the capstone analysis taught in Licence 3 strategic finance modules. Implements `PlaygroundManifest` with category `financial-accounting` and reuses `fmtNumber`, `fmtPct`, and the theme token palette for signal colors.
