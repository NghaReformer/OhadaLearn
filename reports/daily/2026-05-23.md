### MEDIUM — Google Fonts CDN in two legacy HTML playgrounds

**Files:**
- `static/playgrounds/cvp-playground.html:7-9`
- `static/playgrounds/journal-entry-playground.html:7-9`

Both files load fonts from `fonts.googleapis.com`. The main SvelteKit app self-hosts all fonts (per `src/lib/theme/fonts.ts`) and explicitly avoids Google CDN. The iframe playgrounds are therefore making third-party network requests that (a) introduce a GDPR-relevant external dependency, (b) can fail in restricted network environments common in OHADA-zone universities, and (c) are inconsistent with the app's privacy posture. Note: because both playgrounds are now shadowed by their native Svelte counterparts (see above), these files may be unreachable for CVP and journal-entry, but the `depreciation` and `amortization` static HTML files should be checked for the same issue.

**Suggested fix:** Inline the font-face declarations into the static HTML files using the same woff2 paths already hosted under `static/fonts/`, mirroring `src/lib/theme/fonts.ts`.

### Playground 1: Balance Sheet Builder (Bilan SYSCOHADA)

**EN:** SYSCOHADA Balance Sheet Builder  
**FR:** Constructeur de Bilan SYSCOHADA

**Target learner:** Licence 2, Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** Students frequently confuse SYSCOHADA's nine-class account structure with IFRS or French PCG presentation. They sort account balances into the wrong balance sheet section, especially for accounts 18 (related-party long-term debt) vs. 16 (loan from credit institutions) and for accrued income (comptes de régularisation). This playground fixes the misconception that any large balance belongs on the "assets" side by enforcing debit/credit normal balance rules.

**Core interaction:** The learner receives 12–20 randomly generated account balances (drawn from the SYSCOHADA chart, reusing `src/lib/shared/chart-of-accounts`). They drag each account into one of the five SYSCOHADA balance sheet sections: *Actif immobilisé*, *Actif circulant*, *Trésorerie-Actif*, *Capitaux propres et passifs*, *Trésorerie-Passif*. The running total in each section updates live. Submit triggers per-line feedback showing which accounts were misclassified and why.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: set of account balances (pre-seeded scenarios by difficulty)
- Output: completed bilan with subtotals, balance assertion (total assets = total liabilities + equity), colour-coded correctness overlay

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry teaches double-entry mechanics; this teaches what those entries produce — the classified balance sheet. Together they cover the full accounting cycle.

### Playground 2: VAT / TVA Computation (Multi-Regime)

**EN:** OHADA-Zone VAT Calculator  
**FR:** Simulateur TVA Zone OHADA

**Target learner:** Licence 3, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** VAT calculation in OHADA countries is complicated by variable national rates (18 % in Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal, 19.25 % in Cameroon), multiple rate tiers, exempt supplies, and the input-tax credit mechanism. Students conflate the tax-inclusive price formula with the tax-exclusive formula and miscalculate recoverable input TVA. This playground makes the deduction chain visual.

**Core interaction:** Student selects a country (or manual rate), enters purchases (with recoverable / non-recoverable toggle per line) and sales. The tool computes: TVA collectée, TVA déductible, credit or solde dû. A formula panel explains each step. A "What-If" shows the impact of misclassifying one purchase as recoverable.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: country/rate selector, purchase lines (amount, recoverable toggle), sales lines (amount, rate tier)
- Output: TVA liquidation table, net TVA payable or credit balance, formula breakdown

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP addresses cost structure but ignores tax effects. This fills the tax compliance gap that is central to every OHADA-zone SME accounting engagement.

### Playground 3: Payroll Slip Builder (Bulletin de Paie SYSCOHADA)

**EN:** Payroll & Social Charges Calculator  
**FR:** Simulateur Bulletin de Paie

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master 1, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA payroll involves layered employer and employee contributions (CNPS pension, CNPS family allowance, IRPP/income tax with progressive brackets, professional tax where applicable). Students struggle to distinguish gross-to-net from cost-to-employer calculations and to apply the correct bracket table for the jurisdiction. This is a frequent exam topic and a daily professional task.

**Core interaction:** Student enters gross salary, selects country (Cameroon, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire — each has different CNPS rates and IRPP brackets). The tool produces a line-by-line bulletin de paie: gross pay, each social charge line with rate and base, taxable income after deductions, IRPP by bracket, net salary, and employer total cost. Toggle between "compute net from gross" and "back-solve gross from target net" modes.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: gross salary, country, optional allowances (transport, housing)
- Output: full pay slip table with labelled deductions, employer cost summary, IRPP bracket breakdown

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Amortization and interest cover financing costs. This addresses the labour cost dimension of the income statement, completing the cost structure picture started by CVP.

### Playground 4: Accounts Receivable Aging & Provision (Tableau de Vieillissement)

**EN:** Receivables Aging & Doubtful Debt Provision  
**FR:** Tableau de Vieillissement des Créances et Provision

**Target learner:** Licence 2, Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA requires aging analysis to support the *provision pour dépréciation des créances douteuses*. Students often apply a flat provision rate rather than age-based rates, or forget to net the provision against the gross receivable when presenting the bilan. This playground makes the age-bucketing and provision calculation tactile.

**Core interaction:** Student receives 8–15 customer invoices with issue dates. They must classify each into an aging bucket (current, 1–30 d, 31–60 d, 61–90 d, >90 d) by dragging or selecting. Pre-set or adjustable provision rates apply per bucket. The tool computes gross receivables, total provision, and net carrying value, and generates the journal entry for recording the provision.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: set of invoices (amount, issue date), provision rates per bucket (adjustable)
- Output: aging table with subtotals per bucket, provision amount per bucket, total provision, net receivables, auto-generated journal entry (reusing the journal-entry playground's rendering)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Bank Reconciliation handles timing differences on the cash side; this handles the equivalent credit-risk provisioning on the receivables side. Both feed into the bilan's current assets section.

### Playground 5: Financial Ratios Dashboard

**EN:** Financial Ratios Analyser  
**FR:** Tableau de Bord des Ratios Financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master 1

**Pedagogical objective:** Ratio analysis is tested extensively but students calculate ratios in isolation without understanding the interplay (e.g., high liquidity vs. low profitability signals asset idleness). This playground makes the relationships between ratios visible and ties them to SYSCOHADA bilan and compte de résultat structure.

**Core interaction:** Student inputs a simplified SYSCOHADA bilan and compte de résultat (or loads a pre-built scenario). The dashboard computes and visualises 12 ratios grouped by category: *Liquidité* (général, réduit, immédiat), *Solvabilité* (autonomie financière, endettement), *Rentabilité* (ROA, ROE, marge nette), *Gestion* (rotation stocks, DSO, DPO). Colour bands flag ratios as healthy / watch / critical relative to OHADA-zone sector benchmarks. Sliders let the student adjust one line item and see which ratios shift.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: 8–10 summary financial statement fields (total assets, equity, short-term debt, revenue, net income, etc.), optional sector selector
- Output: ratio dashboard with 12 computed values, colour-coded gauge indicators, sensitivity slider showing impact of one-variable change

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP operates at the operational/contribution-margin level; this operates at the strategic/financial-health level. Together they span the management accounting and financial analysis curriculum.
