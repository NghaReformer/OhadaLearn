### HIGH — Missing `--orange` CSS variable in theme tokens
**File:** `src/lib/theme/tokens.ts` (all lines) + 20+ component files  
The theme token set defines `amber` (→ `--amber`), but over 20 components reference `var(--orange, #f59e0b)`. The CSS generator never emits `--orange`, so every usage always falls through to the hardcoded fallback `#f59e0b`. This bypasses the single-source-of-truth principle and means a theme change to `amber` will have no effect on these components.  
**Affected files (sample):** `KpiStrip.svelte:76`, `amortization/ChartPanel.svelte:436`, `bank-reconciliation/VarianceScale.svelte:230`, `ReconciliationFlow.svelte:278`.  
**Fix:** Add `orange` to `ThemeTokens.colors` in `tokens.ts` (value `#f59e0b` or alias to `amber`), regenerate CSS; or replace all `var(--orange, …)` usages with `var(--amber)`.

### HIGH — Empty `aria-labelledby` target on direction radio group
**File:** `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/TransactionJournal.svelte:183–184`  
```svelte
<span class="field-label" id="br-journal-direction">&nbsp;</span>
<div class="direction-toggle" role="radiogroup" aria-labelledby="br-journal-direction">
```
The `radiogroup`'s label element contains only a non-breaking space (`&nbsp;`), so screen readers announce a blank or whitespace label for the inflow/outflow toggle — making it impossible to determine context without surrounding focus. The visible label is intentionally empty to align the toggle with adjacent fields, but the accessible name must not be empty.  
**Fix:** Keep the visual blank by hiding real text with `aria-hidden="true"` on the spacer span and add `aria-label={directionLabel}` directly on the `radiogroup` div instead of `aria-labelledby`. Add an `directionLabel` i18n prop (e.g., `$t('br.journal.direction')`).

### MEDIUM — SVG `<defs>` IDs are document-global and will conflict
**Files:**  
- `bank-reconciliation/VarianceScale.svelte:120,125,129` — IDs `brBeam`, `brPivot`, `brGlow`  
- `bank-reconciliation/MatchingPairsOverlay.svelte:92` — ID `brMatchGlow`  

SVG `id` attributes are scoped to the DOM document, not to the component instance. If either component is ever rendered more than once simultaneously (scenarios tab, multi-panel view, future comparison layouts), duplicate IDs cause the first filter/gradient definition to apply to all instances and the rest to be ignored silently.  
**Fix:** Make IDs unique per instance: generate a prefix from `crypto.randomUUID().slice(0, 8)` in `$state` and interpolate it into each `id=` and `url(#…)` reference.

### LOW — `new Date()` in module scope causes SSR/hydration year mismatch
**File:** `src/lib/components/Footer.svelte:6`  
```ts
let year = new Date().getFullYear();
```
This runs during SSR. If a deploy occurs across a year boundary (Dec 31 → Jan 1) or if the server is in a different timezone, the SSR-rendered year will differ from the hydrated value, producing a console hydration mismatch warning and a stale copyright year until full hydration.  
**Fix:** Move to `$effect` or use `$state` with a browser-only initialiser: `let year = $state(new Date().getFullYear())` is sufficient since Svelte 5 hydration reconciles reactive state; alternately compute it server-side in `+layout.ts` and pass as a prop.

### 1. Financial Statement Ratio Analyser / Tableau de bord de ratios financiers

**EN:** Financial Ratio Dashboard · **FR:** Tableau de Bord des Ratios Financiers  
**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master 1, practicing accountants  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students can compute individual ratios but rarely understand how they interact — e.g., why a high current ratio co-exists with negative free cash flow, or how the SYSCOHADA *fonds de roulement* (FR), *besoin en fonds de roulement* (BFR) and *trésorerie nette* (TN) triangle governs solvency. This playground fixes the mental model of FR/BFR/TN as a connected system, not three isolated numbers.  
**Core interaction:** Learner inputs or selects a simplified SYSCOHADA balance sheet (non-current assets, current assets, equity, long-term liabilities, current liabilities) and an income statement. The playground computes FR, BFR, TN, plus 8–10 OHADA-standard ratios. Key ratios animate as sliders move; a traffic-light panel highlights ratios outside industry norms.  
**MVP inputs/outputs:**  
- Inputs: balance sheet line items, revenue, EBITDA, interest charges  
- Outputs: FR, BFR, TN waterfall diagram; ratio panel with thresholds; what-if: "increase trade receivables by 10% — how does BFR change?"  
**Complements:** Sits upstream of the Bank Reconciliation playground (both deal with cash/liquidity) and downstream of Journal Entry (which generates the balance sheet data).

### 2. Inventory Valuation / Évaluation des Stocks (FIFO vs CMP)

**EN:** Inventory Valuation Methods · **FR:** Méthodes d'Évaluation des Stocks  
**Target learner:** Licence 2  
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA mandates either FIFO or Weighted Average Cost (CMP — Coût Moyen Pondéré); LIFO is prohibited (IAS 2 alignment). Students confuse when to use which, and consistently mis-calculate the CMP after a new purchase lot. This playground makes the running CMP recalculation visible as each transaction is entered.  
**Core interaction:** Learner adds purchase and issue transactions chronologically. Two parallel columns show the stock card computed under FIFO and CMP simultaneously. Each new row highlights exactly which lot(s) were consumed under FIFO, and recalculates the CMP formula step-by-step inline. A summary compares cost of goods sold and ending inventory under each method.  
**MVP inputs/outputs:**  
- Inputs: opening stock, successive purchase lots (qty, unit price), issue quantities  
- Outputs: dual FIFO/CMP stock cards; COGS comparison table; ending inventory valuation; impact on gross margin  
**Complements:** Directly feeds the Journal Entry playground — the ending inventory figure is an account 31xx entry in SYSCOHADA.

### 3. Foreign Currency Transaction Recorder / Opérations en Devises

**EN:** Foreign Currency Transactions · **FR:** Opérations en Devises SYSCOHADA  
**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, professionals in import/export companies  
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA accounts 176 (exchange-rate provisions), 476 (unrealised exchange gains), 477 (unrealised exchange losses) follow specific recognition rules that differ from IFRS. Students consistently book exchange differences in the wrong period or direction. The playground surfaces the difference between transaction-date rate, payment-date rate, and period-end rate.  
**Core interaction:** Learner records a foreign-currency invoice (e.g., EUR-denominated payable for a Cameroonian company reporting in XAF). The playground then advances the timeline — the learner enters the closing rate at period-end and the settlement rate at payment — and the system auto-generates the adjusting entries for accounts 47x, explaining the sign convention at each step.  
**MVP inputs/outputs:**  
- Inputs: functional currency (XAF, XOF, GNF, etc.), transaction currency (EUR, USD, GBP), amounts at three points in time (invoice, year-end, settlement)  
- Outputs: three journal entries with SYSCOHADA account codes; net exchange gain/loss; comparison of provision vs. actual  
**Complements:** Extends the Journal Entry playground into a multi-period scenario; introduces accounts not covered in the existing depreciation or amortization playgrounds.

### 4. Payroll & Charge Sociale Calculator / Calculateur de Paie et Charges Sociales

**EN:** Payroll & Social Charges · **FR:** Bulletin de Paie et Charges Sociales OHADA  
**Target learner:** Licence 2–3, professionals  
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA accounts 66xx (personnel charges) require learners to trace a gross salary through employer social charges, employee deductions, and net pay — and then book the composite journal entry. Most students can compute net pay but cannot reconstruct the dual-sided entry (debit 661/645, credit 421/431/432/433). Different OHADA states use different social-charge rates (CNPS in Cameroon vs. CNSS in Côte d'Ivoire); the playground can expose this variability.  
**Core interaction:** Learner enters gross salary, selects country-specific social-charge regime (configurable rate table), and sees a live payslip alongside the exact SYSCOHADA journal entry for the pay period. A second tab shows the employer's total labour cost vs. the employee's net.  
**MVP inputs/outputs:**  
- Inputs: gross salary, employee category, country preset (Cameroon/Côte d'Ivoire/Senegal/generic)  
- Outputs: annotated payslip; SYSCOHADA journal entry (66x / 421 / 43x); employer total cost breakdown  
**Complements:** Bridges the Journal Entry playground (same entry-posting mechanics) with a domain-specific OHADA workflow that existing playgrounds do not cover.

### 5. Indirect Cash Flow Statement Builder / Tableau des Flux de Trésorerie (Méthode Indirecte)

**EN:** Cash Flow Statement (Indirect Method) · **FR:** Tableau des Flux de Trésorerie — Méthode Indirecte  
**Target learner:** Master 1–2, CPA candidates, professionals  
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA mandates the indirect method for the TFT (Tableau des Flux de Trésorerie), but students who can prepare an income statement often cannot reconcile net income to operating cash flow — especially when non-cash items (depreciation, provisions) and working-capital changes (Δ clients, Δ fournisseurs, Δ stocks) interact. The ordering of adjustments is a persistent source of errors in DSCG-level exams.  
**Core interaction:** Learner is given a simplified income statement and two-period comparative balance sheet. They drag line items into the correct section (operating / investing / financing) and choose the sign convention (add-back vs. deduct) for each adjustment. The running total updates live and a "balance check" confirms the TFT ties to the opening and closing cash balances. Incorrect placements are flagged with a one-sentence explanation of the SYSCOHADA rule.  
**MVP inputs/outputs:**  
- Inputs: net income, depreciation, changes in working-capital accounts, capex, financing flows  
- Outputs: structured three-section TFT; reconciliation to closing cash; per-item feedback on sign errors  
**Complements:** Sits at the top of the learning journey above Journal Entry and Bank Reconciliation — it uses the outputs of those playgrounds (journal entries generate the two-period balance sheet) as its source data.
