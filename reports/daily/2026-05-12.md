- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/ScenarioWalkthrough.svelte:183,231,234,239,271,274`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/BankStatementPanel.svelte:149,209,210`

- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/ReconciliationStatement.svelte:275,279,293,298`

**Explanation:** `css-generator.ts` emits only `--amber`, `--error`, and `--accent` — it does **not** generate `--orange`, `--red`, or `--blue`. Every usage of `var(--orange, #f59e0b)`, `var(--red, #ef4444)`, and `var(--blue, #3b82f6)` therefore always resolves to the hex fallback, completely bypassing the theme. If the theme ever changes its amber or error colors, these components will not update.  
**Fix:** Either (a) alias them in `css-generator.ts` (`--orange: ${c.amber}; --red: ${c.error}; --blue: ${c.accent};`) and add matching fields to `ThemeTokens` in `tokens.ts`, or (b) replace every reference with the already-declared token (`--amber`, `--error`, `--accent`).

**Severity:** MEDIUM

**Explanation:** Running `npm run i18n:check` fails with `sh: tsx: not found`. The `tsx` CLI is not listed as a dev dependency in `package.json`. ### IDEA-01 — VAT / TVA Register
**Title (EN):** VAT Input/Output Register  
**Title (FR):** Registre de TVA collectée / déductible  
**Target learner:** Licence 2 (intro tax accounting)  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students confuse TVA collectée (output VAT, a liability) with TVA déductible (input VAT, an asset) and cannot derive TVA à décaisser. This playground forces them to classify each transaction correctly before showing the net position.  
**Core interaction:** The learner enters a series of sales and purchase invoices (amount HT, TVA rate). Each line is classified as collectée or déductible. A running "TVA account" T-view updates in real time; a period summary panel computes TVA à payer or crédit TVA.  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Invoice amount (HT), rate selector (0 % / 8 % / 18 % / 19.25 % with OHADA-country presets), direction (sale / purchase). Output: TVA collectée subtotal, TVA déductible subtotal, net TVA position, suggested journal entry (471/472 SYSCOHADA accounts).  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The Journal Entry playground teaches the mechanics of recording; this playground teaches the *tax logic* that determines what to record. It naturally extends into the journal entries for TVA settlement.

### IDEA-02 — Financial Ratio Dashboard
**Title (EN):** Financial Ratio Analyzer  
**Title (FR):** Tableau de bord des ratios financiers  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master (financial analysis, audit)  
**Pedagogical objective:** Ratio formulas are memorized in isolation. Learners cannot interpret whether a ratio is good, deteriorating, or sector-outlier without a baseline. This playground pairs ratio calculation with dynamic commentary and sector benchmarks.  
**Core interaction:** The learner inputs two periods of balance sheet and income statement figures (or loads a preset scenario). The panel computes ~15 ratios across four groups (liquidity, solvency, profitability, efficiency). A sparkline shows period-over-period change; a traffic-light indicator flags against SYSCOHADA typical ranges; a "diagnosis" panel generates a short automatic commentary.  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Balance sheet: actif circulant, actif immobilisé, dettes à court terme, capitaux propres, total bilan. Income statement: chiffre d'affaires, résultat d'exploitation, résultat net. Output: current ratio, quick ratio, debt-to-equity, ROE, ROA, ROCE, asset turnover, days receivable/payable, gross margin.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The Journal Entry playground produces statements; this playground teaches what to do with them. CVP covers operational margin; the ratio dashboard extends into a full financial health picture.

### IDEA-03 — Payroll Calculator (Bulletin de paie SYSCOHADA)
**Title (EN):** Payroll & Employer Charges Calculator  
**Title (FR):** Simulateur de bulletin de paie SYSCOHADA  
**Target learner:** Licence 2–3 (social accounting, cost accounting)  
**Pedagogical objective:** Payroll is the most frequent journal entry in any business, yet students struggle to move from gross salary to employer cost and cannot correctly split CNPS employee vs employer shares. This playground makes the full cascade visible.  
**Core interaction:** The learner enters gross salary and selects a country (Cameroon / Côte d'Ivoire / Sénégal / Burkina Faso — covering major OHADA members with different CNPS rates and IRPP brackets). The simulator computes: CNPS employee deduction, CNPS employer contribution, impôt sur le revenu (IRPP), net à payer, and total employer cost. A journal entry panel shows the payroll accounting entries (641x, 431x, 447x accounts).  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Gross salary, country, optional: ancienneté, frais professionnels. Output: payslip breakdown (gross → net), employer cost summary, SYSCOHADA journal entries for salary charge and social contributions.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** This is the missing link between CVP (labor as a cost) and Journal Entry (booking the transaction). No existing playground covers social charges or payroll-specific accounts.

### IDEA-04 — Inventory Valuation Method Comparator
**Title (EN):** Inventory Costing: FIFO vs WAAC  
**Title (FR):** Valorisation des stocks : PEPS vs CMUP  
**Target learner:** Licence 1–2 (introductory financial accounting)  
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA mandates CMUP (weighted average unit cost) as the primary method; many students learn FIFO from international textbooks and do not understand why the choice of method affects reported profit and closing inventory value differently.  
**Core interaction:** The learner enters a sequence of stock entries and exits (quantity + unit cost). The playground renders two synchronized tables — one applying PEPS, one CMUP — with running inventory balance, cost of goods sold per period, and closing stock value. A delta summary panel quantifies the profit impact of each method for the given data set.  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Up to 12 stock movements (date, type: entry/exit, quantity, unit cost for entries). Output: per-method inventory card, COGS, closing stock value, net difference in reported profit.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Depreciation and Amortization cover long-lived asset costs; inventory valuation covers the current-asset cost allocation that drives gross margin on the income statement produced by the Journal Entry playground.

### IDEA-05 — Cash Flow Statement Builder (Indirect Method)
**Title (EN):** Cash Flow Statement — Indirect Method  
**Title (FR):** Tableau de flux de trésorerie — méthode indirecte  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master (financial reporting, audit)  
**Pedagogical objective:** The indirect-method cash flow statement is the most mechanically opaque of the three core financial statements. Students routinely confuse the sign convention (non-cash charges are added back, not deducted) and cannot classify items between operating, investing, and financing activities.  
**Core interaction:** The learner is given a simplified balance sheet (two periods) and an income statement. They drag-and-drop line items into the three activity sections, choosing the sign adjustment for each. The tool validates each classification and sign choice, gives feedback, and renders the completed statement alongside the net change in cash (which must reconcile to the opening/closing cash balances from the balance sheet).  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Comparative balance sheet (~15 line items) and income statement (~8 lines). Output: three-section cash flow statement, running total per section, final reconciliation check, and color-coded validation overlay.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** The Journal Entry playground generates the income statement and balance sheet; this playground teaches students to derive the third mandatory statement from those two, completing the full SYSCOHADA financial reporting cycle.
