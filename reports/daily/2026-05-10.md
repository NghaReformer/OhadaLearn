### P-1 — Inventory Valuation: FIFO vs. WAAC

**EN:** Inventory Valuation (FIFO / Weighted Average)
**FR:** Valorisation des Stocks (FIFO / CMUP)

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA restricts inventory costing to FIFO and the Weighted Average Cost method (CMUP — Coût Moyen Unitaire Pondéré); LIFO is explicitly prohibited. Students routinely confuse the two methods and underestimate how CMUP is recalculated after each receipt. This playground fixes that.

**Core interaction:** Learner builds a stock card (fiche de stock) by entering a sequence of purchases and sales; the tool computes the running CMUP (or FIFO layers) after each transaction and renders a full stock ledger showing unit cost, total cost, and closing stock value. A side-by-side comparison panel shows the profit impact of choosing each method.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: opening stock (qty, unit cost), n purchase lines (qty, price), n sale lines (qty)
- Outputs: full stock ledger table, cost of goods sold, closing inventory value, CMUP after each receipt, method comparison strip

**Complement:** The journal-entry playground handles the bookkeeping journal; this playground teaches the upstream cost computation that feeds the journal. Together they close the inventory accounting cycle.

### P-2 — TVA Mechanics & Recovery Rate

**EN:** VAT Calculator & Recovery Rate
**FR:** Calculateur TVA & Taux de Déduction

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone VAT rates differ by country (18% in Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal; 19.25% in Cameroun pre-reform; 20% in others). Mixed-use businesses (taxable + exempt turnover) must compute a *prorata* recovery rate. Students and junior accountants routinely over-claim input VAT because they skip the prorata calculation.

**Core interaction:** Learner selects their OHADA country (driving the applicable standard rate), enters taxable and exempt turnover, and enters purchase invoices. The tool computes: output VAT due, recoverable input VAT (with prorata), net VAT payable or credit, and the monthly TVA return schedule (ligne par ligne).

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: country (rate lookup), taxable turnover, exempt turnover, purchase lines (amount, VAT rate, category)
- Outputs: prorata rate, deductible input VAT, net VAT payable/credit, summary declaration table

**Complement:** No existing playground touches tax computation. This is the most requested topic by OHADA practitioners in the waitlist survey segment.

### P-3 — SYSCOHADA Financial Statement Assembly

**EN:** Financial Statement Builder (SYSCOHADA)
**FR:** Assemblage des États Financiers (SYSCOHADA)

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master

**Pedagogical objective:** The SYSCOHADA Bilan (balance sheet) and Compte de Résultat (P&L) follow a specific two-column format and classification order that differs from IFRS. Students who master journal entries still struggle to produce correctly formatted statutory statements. This playground bridges the gap.

**Core interaction:** The learner is given a pre-filled trial balance (or can load the output from the journal-entry playground). They drag account balances into the correct rows of the SYSCOHADA Bilan and Compte de Résultat. The tool validates classification, flags misplacements, and computes final totals and key ratios (fonds de roulement, besoin en fonds de roulement, trésorerie nette).

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: trial balance (account codes + balances, pre-populated or imported)
- Outputs: SYSCOHADA Bilan, Compte de Résultat, FRN/BFR/TN ratios, mismatch warnings

**Complement:** This is the downstream stage of the journal-entry playground, completing the full accounting cycle. It also links to the chart-of-accounts page for classification reference.

### P-4 — Payroll & Social Charges (CNPS / IRPP)

**EN:** Payroll Calculator — OHADA Zone
**FR:** Simulateur de Paie — Zone OHADA

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master / HR & finance professionals

**Pedagogical objective:** CNPS (Caisse Nationale de Prévoyance Sociale) contribution rates, IRPP/IRNS salary tax brackets, and the distinction between employer cost and net pay are poorly understood even by practitioners. The calculation is highly country-specific within the OHADA zone.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters gross salary, employment category, country, and family situation. The tool computes: employee CNPS contributions (old-age, family allowances, occupational risks), employer CNPS contributions, IRPP/IRNS tax withholding (bracket-by-bracket), net pay, total employer cost, and payslip summary. A toggle shows the employer accounting journal entry.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: country (Cameroun, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal, or generic), gross salary, category (cadre/non-cadre), dependants
- Outputs: payslip breakdown table, employer cost summary, optional journal entry

**Complement:** No existing playground addresses labour cost. Payroll is a required topic in every OHADA accounting curriculum and a high-frequency task for practitioners.

### P-5 — Doubtful Debt Provision (Créances Douteuses)

**EN:** Accounts Receivable Provision
**FR:** Provisionnement des Créances Douteuses

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA requires provisioning trade receivables when recovery is uncertain, using a specific procedure: reclassification to account 416, then a provision entry in account 491. Students confuse write-off with provisioning, and routinely omit the VAT adjustment on the doubtful portion (since VAT credit can only be reclaimed on a written-off, not a merely doubtful, debt).

**Core interaction:** Learner enters an aged receivables schedule (customer, invoice date, amount, likelihood of recovery %). The tool computes the required provision (gross vs. net-of-VAT base), generates the provision journal entries (accounts 6911 / 491 / 4431), and shows the reversal entry if the debt is subsequently recovered or written off.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: receivable lines (amount, VAT rate, recovery %), existing provision balance
- Outputs: provision journal entries, closing 491 balance, sensitivity to recovery % slider, write-off comparison

**Complement:** Links naturally to the journal-entry playground (learners can replay the computed entries there) and to the financial statement builder (bad debt provisions affect both the balance sheet and P&L). The VAT angle adds complexity absent from any existing playground.
