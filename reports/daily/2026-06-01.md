### 2.1 SYSCOHADA Balance Sheet Builder *(Constructeur de Bilan)*

**EN:** SYSCOHADA Balance Sheet Builder | **FR:** Constructeur de Bilan SYSCOHADA  
**Target learner:** Licence 2, Licence 3  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students confuse the SYSCOHADA balance sheet layout (Classes 1–5 in a specific left/right mapping) with the IFRS or French-PCG layout. This playground fixes the misconception that "assets = left" is universal — SYSCOHADA has fixed structural zones (Actif Immobilisé, Actif Circulant / Passif Capitaux Propres, Dettes Financières, Passif Circulant) that must be understood before the financial statements module.  
**Core interaction:** Learner receives a trial balance (20–30 accounts) and drags each account into its correct balance sheet zone. Instant feedback: a running totals panel shows whether Actif = Passif after each placement. A "reveal structure" toggle shows the official SYSCOHADA form.  
**Inputs:** Pre-seeded trial balance with SYSCOHADA chart of accounts numbers (Class 1–5). **Outputs:** Completed bilan with zone totals, equity check indicator, and a score (accounts correctly placed / total).  
**Complements existing work:** The journal-entry playground stops at the ledger level. This playground bridges ledger → financial statement, completing the accounting cycle.

### 2.2 TVA / VAT Flow Calculator *(Calculateur de Flux TVA)*

**EN:** OHADA-Zone VAT Flow Calculator | **FR:** Calculateur de Flux TVA Zone OHADA  
**Target learner:** Licence 2, practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students and junior accountants frequently miscalculate TVA à décaisser (VAT payable) by failing to net TVA collectée against TVA déductible, or by mishandling prorata rules for mixed-activity entities. Country-specific rates (18 % in Cameroon/Côte d'Ivoire/Sénégal, 19.25 % in Cameroon for certain regimes) add further confusion.  
**Core interaction:** Learner enters a list of purchase and sale invoices with amounts HT (excluding tax). The playground computes TVA collectée, TVA déductible, and TVA à décaisser (or crédit de TVA). A collapsible "Declaration Preview" panel shows the CERFA-style declaration layout used in OHADA countries.  
**Inputs:** Invoice rows (date, description, HT amount, TTC amount, type: purchase/sale), country selector (to pick rate). **Outputs:** TVA collectée total, TVA déductible total, net TVA position, prorata coefficient input if needed.  
**Complements existing work:** Bank reconciliation deals with cash flows; this playground deals with tax cash flows and declaration mechanics — a distinct skill gap in the curriculum.

### 2.3 Payroll Bulletin Builder *(Constructeur de Bulletin de Paie)*

**EN:** OHADA Payroll Bulletin Builder | **FR:** Constructeur de Bulletin de Paie OHADA  
**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** Personnel costs (accounts 66X in SYSCOHADA) require understanding gross-to-net calculation: CNPS employee share, IRPP withholding, employer CNPS, and FDFP training levy. The mechanics differ by country (Cameroon vs. Côte d'Ivoire vs. Sénégal) and most students have never built a bulletin from scratch.  
**Core interaction:** Learner enters gross salary and selects country. The playground computes all deductions step-by-step in a vertical bulletin layout (exactly mirroring a real pay slip). Each line item is expandable to show the applicable rate and calculation formula.  
**Inputs:** Gross monthly salary, country, employment category (cadre/non-cadre), optional transport allowance, optional housing allowance. **Outputs:** Net salary, employer cost, CNPS contributions (employer + employee), IRPP, a journal entry stub for account 66X and 43X.  
**Complements existing work:** Journal entry playground can import the output of this playground in Phase 2 via the `SharedResourceType` plugin contract, creating a natural learning sequence.

### 2.5 Trade Receivables Aging & Provision *(Balance Âgée et Provisions)*

**EN:** Receivables Aging & Doubtful Accounts Provision | **FR:** Balance Âgée et Provisions pour Créances Douteuses  
**Target learner:** Licence 3, practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA (accounts 416, 491) requires provisioning doubtful receivables using aging brackets and applying the net-of-provision presentation rule. Students frequently confuse gross receivable, provision, and net book value; they also mispost the allowance entry (debit 6911 / credit 491, not a direct write-off of 411).  
**Core interaction:** Learner enters a table of open invoices (customer, invoice date, amount, collection status: current / 30–90 days / 90–180 days / >180 days). The playground applies a configurable provision rate schedule (e.g., 25 % at 90 days, 50 % at 180 days, 100 % beyond) and produces (1) the aging grid, (2) the required provision amount, (3) the adjusting journal entry, and (4) the balance sheet presentation.  
**Inputs:** Invoice rows, provision rate schedule (editable), prior-year provision balance (for the delta entry). **Outputs:** Aging bucket totals, gross/provision/net receivables, debit 6911 / credit 491 entry, and an explanation of the SYSCOHADA presentation rules.  
**Complements existing work:** Bank reconciliation handles the cash side of receivables; this playground handles the accrual and provisioning side — the two together cover the full receivables lifecycle.
