### BUG-01 — Missing `--orange`, `--blue`, `--red` CSS variables in theme generator
**Severity:** High  
**Files (first occurrence per file):**
- `src/lib/components/playground/KpiStrip.svelte:76`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/amortization/components/ChartPanel.svelte:436`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/amortization/components/InputsPanel.svelte:604`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/amortization/components/ScheduleTable.svelte:240`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/BankStatementPanel.svelte:175`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/LedgerPanel.svelte:175`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/ReconciliationFlow.svelte:278`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/ScenarioWalkthrough.svelte:234`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/TransactionJournal.svelte:382`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/VarianceScale.svelte:230`

**Explanation:** `src/lib/theme/css-generator.ts` emits `--green`, `--amber`, `--error`, and `--accent` but never `--orange`, `--blue`, or `--red`. All 24+ usages of `var(--orange, #f59e0b)`, `var(--blue, #3b82f6)`, and `var(--red, #ef4444)` across 10 files therefore always resolve to their hardcoded fallbacks — the CSS variable mechanism provides zero theming benefit. The design tokens have `--amber` for orange-adjacent use and `--error` for red; `--blue` has no equivalent at all.

**Suggested fix:** Add `--orange: ${c.amber};`, `--blue: ${c.accent};`, `--red: ${c.error};` aliases to `css-generator.ts` (or rename usages to `--amber`/`--error`). Add `orange` to `ThemeTokens.colors` in `tokens.ts` if a distinct value from amber is intended.

### BUG-03 — Privacy page content is hardcoded English — no i18n
**Severity:** High  
**File:** `src/routes/[lang]/privacy/+page.svelte:10–27`

**Explanation:** The entire privacy policy body (`<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<p>` content, and the "Last updated" date) is hardcoded English HTML with no `$t()` calls. A French user at `/fr/privacy` sees fully English text. The file imports `$lib/i18n` but uses it nowhere. This is the only route that completely bypasses the i18n system.

**Suggested fix:** Create `src/lib/i18n/namespaces/privacy.en.ts` and `privacy.fr.ts`, register them in `src/lib/i18n/index.ts`, then replace all prose with `$t('privacy.*')` keys. Run `npm run i18n:check` afterward to regenerate types.

## 2. New Playground Ideas

The current native playground inventory covers: TVM, CVP, Journal Entry, Amortization (loan), Depreciation, Bank Reconciliation, and Interest rates. All five proposals below target gaps in the SYSCOHADA curriculum not covered by existing playgrounds.

---

### IDEA-01 — Financial Statements Builder
**Title (EN):** Financial Statements Builder  
**Title (FR):** Constructeur d'états financiers  
**Target learner:** Licence 2–3, practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students can memorize journal entries but fail to see how the trial balance transforms into the SYSCOHADA balance sheet (Bilan) and income statement (Compte de résultat). This playground closes the gap between transactional bookkeeping and period-end reporting by making the classification step visible and reversible.  
**Core interaction:** The learner is given a pre-populated trial balance with 15–20 accounts. They drag each account row into the correct section of a two-column SYSCOHADA Bilan or Compte de résultat template. Immediate feedback highlights misclassifications (e.g., putting `Clients` in equity). A "Check" action reveals the correct statement and highlights each delta.  
**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: a trial balance dataset (debit/credit balances per account)
- Output: a completed SYSCOHADA-formatted balance sheet and income statement; a score showing % of accounts correctly placed
**Complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry teaches double-entry recording; this playground is the logical next step — taking those entries to financial closure. Pairs naturally with the existing Journal Entry playground's exercise data.

---

### IDEA-02 — VAT / TVA Declaration Simulator
**Title (EN):** VAT Declaration Simulator  
**Title (FR):** Simulateur de déclaration TVA  
**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** TVA (Value Added Tax) computations are universally required in OHADA-zone businesses but students consistently confuse collectée vs. déductible, struggle with mixed-rate transactions, and cannot reconcile the amounts on a declaration form. This playground builds muscle memory for the OHADA TVA regime.  
**Core interaction:** The learner enters a month's worth of purchases and sales (amount HT, TVA rate: 0%, 10%, 18%, 19.25% depending on country). The simulator calculates TVA collectée, TVA déductible, and the net payable or credit to carry forward. A "declaration preview" renders the entries on a stylized tax form. A "What-If" panel lets learners toggle between TVA rates by OHADA member country.  
**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: purchase lines (amount, rate) and sales lines (amount, rate), country selector
- Output: TVA collectée, TVA déductible, solde net, declaration form visual  
**Complements existing playgrounds:** The Interest playground handles time-value computations; this is the first playground in the tax domain, which is a major gap for a B2B platform targeting practicing accountants.

---

### IDEA-03 — Inventory Valuation Methods
**Title (EN):** Inventory Valuation Methods  
**Title (FR):** Méthodes d'évaluation des stocks  
**Target learner:** Licence 2, Licence 3  
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA permits FIFO and weighted average cost (CMUP) but not LIFO. Students consistently mis-apply CMUP by recalculating it too early or late in the period. This playground isolates and visualizes the timing dependency.  
**Core interaction:** The learner enters a sequence of stock movements (initial balance, purchases with quantity+cost, sales with quantity). They select either FIFO or CMUP. The playground renders a running stock card (fiche de stock) showing unit cost, quantity, total value after each movement, and the resulting COGS and closing stock value. Switching methods side-by-side shows the difference in profit under each approach.  
**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: opening stock (qty, unit cost), a list of purchase and sale movements
- Output: a formatted stock card (fiche de stock), closing value, COGS, operating profit comparison across methods  
**Complements existing playgrounds:** Depreciation covers asset consumption; this covers current-asset valuation — together they address both sides of the asset section in the Bilan.

---

### IDEA-04 — Payroll Calculator (SYSCOHADA / OHADA zone)
**Title (EN):** Payroll Calculator  
**Title (FR):** Calculateur de fiche de paie  
**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** Payroll in OHADA-zone countries involves CNPS (social contributions), IRPP (personal income tax with a progressive schedule), and professional tax — each country has different rates. Students cannot produce a correct net-pay calculation without a structured walkthrough of each deduction layer.  
**Core interaction:** The learner enters gross salary, number of dependants, country (Cameroun, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal — pre-loaded rate tables). The playground steps through: gross → CNPS employee deduction → taxable base → IRPP brackets → other deductions → net. Each line is annotated with the formula used. A toggle lets learners see both employer and employee costs side by side.  
**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: gross salary, dependants, country
- Output: itemized pay slip (bulletin de paie) with each deduction labeled; employer total cost  
**Complements existing playgrounds:** This introduces labor accounting — a domain entirely absent from the current playground set — and directly serves the "practicing professional" segment of the B2B2C model.

---

### IDEA-05 — Cash Flow Statement Builder (Indirect Method)
**Title (EN):** Cash Flow Statement — Indirect Method  
**Title (FR):** Tableau des flux de trésorerie — méthode indirecte  
**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master  
**Pedagogical objective:** The indirect method starts from net income and adjusts for non-cash items; students routinely add depreciation in the wrong direction, mis-classify investing/financing flows, and end up with a closing cash balance that doesn't match the Bilan. SYSCOHADA mandates the indirect method for the Tableau de financement.  
**Core interaction:** The learner is given a simplified set of comparative balance sheets and an income statement (pre-loaded scenario). They drag and classify each balance sheet change into one of three sections (operating, investing, financing) and choose the sign (add/subtract). The playground recalculates the cash flow total in real time and shows whether it reconciles with the change in the cash position on the Bilan. A "hints" mode reveals the SYSCOHADA classification rules for each line.  
**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: comparative Bilan (two periods) and Compte de résultat (net income, depreciation, gains/losses)
- Output: a three-section SYSCOHADA Tableau des flux de trésorerie with a reconciliation check  
**Complements existing playgrounds:** The Financial Statements Builder (IDEA-01) covers period-end preparation; this playground is the capstone — connecting income, balance sheet changes, and liquidity in a single integrated view.
