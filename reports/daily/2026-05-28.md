## 1. Bugs & Issues

### 1.1 Undefined CSS Custom Properties (27 usages) — **HIGH**

The theme's `css-generator.ts` does **not** emit `--orange`, `--blue`, or `--red`. Components across the codebase reference these variables with hardcoded hex fallbacks, which means the fallback values are what actually renders. This breaks the "one token file to restyle everything" promise stated in CLAUDE.md.

| Variable used | Correct token | Files (representative) |
|---|---|---|
| `var(--orange, #f59e0b)` | `var(--amber)` | `ScheduleTable.svelte:240`, `KpiStrip.svelte:76`, `ChartPanel.svelte:436`, `InputsPanel.svelte:604` (amortization) |
| `var(--blue, #3b82f6)` | `var(--accent)` | `ScheduleTable.svelte:246`, `ChartPanel.svelte:444` |
| `var(--red, #ef4444)` | `var(--error)` | `ScheduleTable.svelte:259`, `InputsPanel.svelte:605` |
| `var(--green, #22c55e)` | `var(--green)` (correct name, wrong fallback shade — token is `#34d399`) | `ScheduleTable.svelte:265`, `ReconciliationStatement.svelte:275,293` |

**Suggested fix:** Replace every `var(--orange, …)` → `var(--amber)`, `var(--blue, …)` → `var(--accent)`, `var(--red, …)` → `var(--error)`. Drop the fallback argument — if the CSS var isn't set, the page has a larger theming bug that should surface visibly.

### 2.1 Payroll Accounting / Bulletin de Paie

**EN:** Payroll & Social Charges Calculator  
**FR:** Calculateur de bulletin de paie et charges sociales

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3, practicing HR accountants

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone employers face complex layering of gross salary, CNPS (social security) contributions, IRPP withholding, and transport/meal allowances — all using accounts 661x/64x in SYSCOHADA. Students systematically confuse the employer portion of charges from the employee portion, and mispost provisions vs. actual payments. This playground forces them to separate the three flows: (1) gross payroll journal entry, (2) employer charges accrual, (3) cash settlement.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters gross salary, contract type, and jurisdiction (Cameroon/Côte d'Ivoire/Senegal — different CNPS rates). The playground computes net pay, deductions, and employer charges, then generates the SYSCOHADA journal entries in real time. A confirmation step asks the learner to categorise each line before revealing the correct posting.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Gross salary, employee count, jurisdiction selector  
**Outputs:** Payslip summary table, journal entries (661x, 431x, 447x, 5xx), CNPS / IRPP breakdown

**Why it complements existing:** Journal Entry teaches the mechanics of a posting but uses generic scenarios. This grounds the same mechanics in a concrete, high-frequency OHADA transaction class that no other playground covers.

### 2.2 VAT / TVA Position Calculator

**EN:** VAT Position & Tax Journal  
**FR:** Position TVA et journal fiscal

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master 1, tax-track professionals

**Pedagogical objective:** Students conflate deductible VAT (on purchases, account 4452) with collected VAT (on sales, account 4431), and cannot compute the net VAT payable / refundable each month. The OHADA zone uses 18–19.25 % standard rates with sector-specific exemptions; the netting rule is consistently misapplied.

**Core interaction:** Learner records 4–8 purchase and sale invoices by dragging them into the correct category. The playground shows running totals for TVA collectée, TVA déductible, and net position. An "Arrêté du mois" (month close) step generates the settlement journal entry (account 4441 or 4452 contra) and flags any non-deductible lines.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Invoice list (amount, tax rate, purchase/sale toggle, deductible yes/no)  
**Outputs:** TVA position statement, settlement journal entry, month-end balance on 443x/445x accounts

**Why it complements existing:** No existing playground touches tax compliance. This fills the single largest gap in the SYSCOHADA Licence 3 curriculum.

---

### 2.3 Inventory Valuation Methods (FIFO / WMAC)

**EN:** Inventory Costing: FIFO vs WMAC  
**FR:** Méthodes de valorisation des stocks : FIFO vs CMP

**Target learner:** Licence 2, Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA mandates either FIFO or Weighted-Moving-Average Cost (CMP — coût moyen pondéré) for stock valuation. Students cannot see how the choice affects cost of goods sold (account 6031) and closing stock (account 3x), especially under price fluctuation. They also confuse perpetual vs periodic inventory updates.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a sequence of stock receipts (quantity + unit cost) and issues. Two parallel columns run FIFO and WMAC simultaneously, updated on each entry. A summary bar compares the impact on gross margin and closing stock value.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Up to 12 stock movements (receipt / issue, quantity, unit price), display mode (perpetual / periodic)  
**Outputs:** Valuation table, COGS comparison, closing stock comparison, journal entries (6031, 31xx)

**Why it complements existing:** Bridges the Amortization playground's "historical cost" mindset into inventory — a topic that directly feeds the income statement used in the CVP playground.

---

### 2.4 Financial Ratio Dashboard

**EN:** Financial Ratios & Diagnosis  
**FR:** Ratios financiers et diagnostic d'entreprise

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master 1, financial analysts

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can compute individual ratios from a formula sheet but cannot interpret the result set as a coherent financial picture. The SYSCOHADA balance sheet structure (actif immobilisé, actif circulant, trésorerie actif; capitaux propres, dettes financières, passif circulant, trésorerie passif) differs enough from IFRS that generic ratio tutorials mislead.

**Core interaction:** Learner loads a pre-built SYSCOHADA balance sheet / income statement (or modifies it with sliders). The playground displays a set of 10 standard ratios grouped by family (liquidity, solvency, profitability, activity) with traffic-light colouring. Changing a single line item updates all ratios instantly, teaching sensitivity.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Simplified SYSCOHADA balance sheet (8 line items) and income statement (5 line items); year-2 comparison toggle  
**Outputs:** Ratio table with benchmarks, spider chart, written diagnostic summary (templated, bilingual)

**Why it complements existing:** The CVP playground covers internal cost analysis; this playground adds external financial analysis, completing the Licence 3 financial analysis module. It also reuses the shared Chart of Accounts structure already in `src/lib/shared/chart-of-accounts/`.

### 2.5 Cash Flow Statement Builder (Indirect Method)

**EN:** Cash Flow Statement — Indirect Method  
**FR:** Tableau des flux de trésorerie — méthode indirecte

**Target learner:** Master 1, practicing accountants

**Pedagogical objective:** The SYSCOHADA indirect method cash flow statement (TAFIRE — Tableau de financement) confuses nearly every Licence-level student because it starts from net income and adjusts for non-cash items in a counter-intuitive direction. Students routinely reverse the sign on depreciation (account 28xx) and working capital changes (∆BFR).

**Core interaction:** Learner is given a simplified income statement and two-year balance sheet. They must classify each adjustment item as operating / investing / financing and assign the correct sign. The playground tracks a running total for each section and reveals sign errors inline before the learner moves to the next step. A final reconciliation step checks that the net change in cash matches the balance sheet movement in account 52x.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Net income, 4 non-cash expense items, 3 working capital changes, 2 investing flows, 1 financing flow  
**Outputs:** Completed TAFIRE, sign-error annotations, cash reconciliation proof, journal for settlement of financing items

**Why it complements existing:** The Journal Entry playground teaches individual postings; TVM covers time-value concepts. Neither teaches how cash moves at the entity level over a year. The TAFIRE is a statutory filing requirement in 17 OHADA countries, making this one of the highest-impact additions possible.
