### IDEA-01 · SYSCOHADA Balance Sheet Builder / Constructeur de bilan SYSCOHADA

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** Students consistently confuse the SYSCOHADA actif/passif structure (immobilisations → actif circulant → trésorerie actif; capitaux propres → dettes financières → passif circulant → trésorerie passif) with French PCG or IFRS layouts. This playground fixes that by requiring the learner to classify and slot every account.

**Core interaction:** Given a pre-populated trial balance (soldes) for a fictional OHADA-zone enterprise, the learner drags accounts into the correct section of the two-column SYSCOHADA bilan (actif left, passif right). Real-time totals update and the interface flags actif ≠ passif imbalances. A "reveal" mode shows the authoritative layout.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Input: 15–20 pre-seeded account balances (editable amounts)
- Output: formatted bilan with section subtotals, actif total = passif total check, error highlights for misclassified accounts

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal-entry teaches recording; bank-reconciliation teaches adjustments; this bridges to the final financial statement. No other existing playground produces a classified balance sheet.

### IDEA-02 · TVA (Value-Added Tax) Calculator / Simulateur de TVA

**Target learner:** Licence 2, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** VAT in OHADA-zone countries uses the invoice method with specific rules around deductible input tax, prorata for mixed-use companies, and monthly filing. Students routinely mis-calculate the TVA à décaisser / à récupérer or apply the wrong prorata. This playground gives immediate feedback on each step.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a month's sales invoices (HT + TVA collectée) and purchase invoices (HT + TVA déductible), including one or two exempt items to force a prorata calculation. The playground computes TVA collectée, TVA déductible (with prorata applied), and the net TVA à payer or crédit de TVA. Toggle between OHADA-zone country-specific rates (Cameroon 19.25 %, Côte d'Ivoire 18 %, Sénégal 18 %, etc.).

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: country selector (drives rate), N sales lines, N purchase lines, exempt sales fraction
- Outputs: TVA collectée, TVA déductible, prorata %, net TVA payable, a visual comparison bar of collected vs deductible

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP and TVM deal with pre-tax figures; interest handles borrowing costs. TVA is the most frequently encountered compliance calculation in OHADA practice and is entirely absent from the current catalog.

### IDEA-03 · Financial Ratios Dashboard / Tableau de bord des ratios financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can compute individual ratios mechanically but struggle to read a cohort of ratios together as a diagnostic story. The playground builds the interpretation muscle: given a company's simplified bilan + compte de résultat, compute and benchmark each ratio class against OHADA-zone sector norms.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters (or selects a scenario preset) key line items from bilan and résultat: total actif, actif circulant, stocks, créances, trésorerie, capitaux propres, dettes totales, CA, résultat net, EBE (gross operating surplus). The dashboard renders four ratio families — liquidité, solvabilité, rentabilité, activité — as gauges with color-coded traffic-light ratings against editable sector benchmarks. A "what-if" slider lets the learner adjust one input and watch all affected ratios cascade.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: 12–15 financial statement line items, sector benchmark overrides
- Outputs: 10–12 named ratios, gauge visualizations, a short auto-generated diagnostic paragraph (templated, i18n-safe)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Bank-reconciliation produces a reconciled book balance; CVP gives operational breakeven. This is the synthesis layer — reading the complete financial health of an entity — which no existing playground addresses.

### IDEA-04 · Inventory Valuation Methods / Méthodes de valorisation des stocks

**Target learner:** Licence 2

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA permits CMUP (coût moyen unitaire pondéré) calculated either at each exit or at period end, plus FIFO. Students systematically confuse the two CMUP variants and cannot explain why the ending stock value differs across methods. This playground makes the difference visceral.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a sequence of stock movements (date, type: entrée/sortie, quantity, unit cost for entrées). The playground calculates closing stock value and cost of goods sold simultaneously under FIFO, CMUP-fin-de-période, and CMUP-après-chaque-mouvement, displaying the three results side-by-side in color-coded columns. Each row shows the running calculation so the learner can trace exactly how each method diverges.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: up to 20 movement rows (date, entrée/sortie, qty, unit cost)
- Outputs: per-method table (FIFO, CMUP-période, CMUP-mouvement), ending stock value, COGS, and a δ-difference highlight

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Amortization covers asset cost allocation; depreciation covers fixed-asset write-down. Inventory valuation fills the gap for current-asset cost flow — the third major cost allocation topic in SYSCOHADA financial accounting.

### IDEA-05 · Payroll & Social Charges Calculator / Simulateur de bulletin de paie

**Target learner:** Master, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Computing net-from-gross pay in OHADA-zone countries is notoriously error-prone because social security rates (CNPS/CRTV), income tax brackets (IRPP/impôt sur le revenu), and employer surcharges differ by country and change frequently. No existing tool gives students a country-parameterized, step-by-step breakdown.

**Core interaction:** Learner selects country (Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal — editable rate tables), enters gross monthly salary, number of dependants, and optional allowances (logement, transport). The playground steps through: cotisations salariales (CNPS part salarié), cotisations patronales (CNPS part patronal), base taxable IRPP, IRPP computation (marginal brackets shown), and net à payer. A summary table maps directly to the five main lines of the bulletin de paie that employers must issue under OHADA labour law.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: country selector (drives all rates), gross salary, dependants, allowance amounts
- Outputs: itemised deduction table, IRPP bracket visualization, net salary, total employer cost (charge totale employeur)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** TVM and amortization cover financing decisions. Payroll is the most common accounting task in SME practice and touches SYSCOHADA class-6 wage expense entries — closing the loop back to the journal-entry playground's recorded transactions.
