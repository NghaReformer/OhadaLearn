### A. Working Capital Simulator / Simulateur BFR

**Title (FR):** Simulateur du Besoin en Fonds de Roulement (BFR)  
**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students consistently confuse FRNG (fonds de roulement net global), BFR, and trésorerie nette. They can memorize the formulas but cannot reason about *why* a profitable company can be cash-starved. This playground makes the cash-conversion cycle tactile.  
**Core interaction:** Learner adjusts five sliders — Days Sales Outstanding, Days Inventory Outstanding, Days Payable Outstanding, revenue, and growth rate — and watches BFR, FRNG, and net cash react in real time on a waterfall chart. A color-coded warning fires when BFR > FRNG (liquidity stress zone).  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** DSO, DIO, DPO (days), annual revenue (FCFA), FRNG (FCFA) → BFR (FCFA), trésorerie nette, cash-stress indicator, breakdown bar chart.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Amortization models *debt repayment*; this models *operational cash needs*. Together they give a complete picture of why financing decisions matter beyond just the interest rate.

### B. Financial Ratio Dashboard / Tableau de Bord des Ratios Financiers

**Title (FR):** Tableau de Bord des Ratios SYSCOHADA  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students learn ratio formulas in isolation and forget their interpretive thresholds. This playground teaches diagnosis — reading a company's financial health from its SYSCOHADA balance sheet and income statement using the Centrale de Bilans methodology.  
**Core interaction:** Learner enters SYSCOHADA bilan and compte de résultat line items (structured as labeled numeric inputs matching SYSCOHADA account classes). The playground computes ~15 ratios grouped into liquidity, solvency, profitability, and activity categories, and displays each with a gauge showing whether it falls in a healthy/watch/critical band calibrated for the OHADA region.  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Actif immobilisé, actif circulant, dettes court terme, capitaux propres, chiffre d'affaires, résultat net → ratios de liquidité générale, autonomie financière, rentabilité économique, rotation des stocks, etc., each with a benchmark band.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground interprets a complete financial picture. Journal-entry teaches *recording*; this teaches *reading*.

### C. VAT Return Builder / Déclaration de TVA

**Title (FR):** Simulateur de Déclaration de TVA  
**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone practitioners must file monthly/quarterly TVA returns but students conflate TVA collectée, TVA déductible, TVA sur immobilisations, and the credit-report mechanism. Errors on real returns carry penalties.  
**Core interaction:** Learner inputs a set of simulated purchase invoices (with selectable TVA rates: 18% Cameroun, 18% Sénégal, 19.25% Côte d'Ivoire, etc.) and sale invoices for a given period. The playground builds the return table line by line — TVA collectée, TVA déductible (biens/services), TVA déductible (immobilisations), net TVA dû / crédit à reporter — and shows the final settlement or credit carry-forward.  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** List of purchase/sale transactions with amounts and TVA applicability → TVA collectée total, TVA déductible total, TVA nette, crédit reportable.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Bank reconciliation handles cash flows; this handles the tax obligation side of the same transactions. Together they cover the full monthly accounting cycle.

### D. Budget Variance Analyzer / Analyse des Écarts Budgétaires

**Title (FR):** Analyse des Écarts Budgétaires  
**Target learner:** Master / Practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** Management accounting students in the OHADA zone learn écart sur coût, écart sur volume, and écart sur prix in theory but struggle to decompose a variance into its components and interpret the managerial signal correctly.  
**Core interaction:** Learner enters budgeted and actual figures for a cost center — standard cost per unit, budgeted volume, actual cost per unit, actual volume. The playground decomposes the total variance into écart sur prix, écart sur quantité, and écart sur volume via a waterfall chart and flags whether each is favorable or unfavorable with a one-sentence managerial interpretation.  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Budget: cost/unit, volume. Actual: cost/unit, volume → écart total, écart sur prix, écart sur quantité, écart sur activité, waterfall visualization, F/U flags.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP models profit sensitivity to volume *prospectively*; this analyzes *retrospective* variance against a budget. They teach opposite ends of the management cycle.

### E. Payroll Calculator / Calculateur de Paie OHADA

**Title (FR):** Calculateur de Bulletin de Paie  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** Payroll computation in OHADA-zone countries involves country-specific social charge schedules (CNPS, CNAMGS, IPM rates differ between Cameroun, Gabon, Sénégal, etc.). Students have no tools to practice gross-to-net calculations with real-world rates, so they arrive in firms unable to verify payroll outputs.  
**Core interaction:** Learner selects country, enters gross salary, seniority years, and transportation/meal allowances. The playground computes employee deductions (cotisations sociales, IRPP tranche, pension) and employer charges (CNPS patronal, accidents de travail, allocations familiales) step by step, outputting a formatted bulletin de paie with each line labeled using SYSCOHADA payroll account codes (Class 6 — 661/662/663).  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Country (dropdown), salaire brut, ancienneté, avantages → salaire net, IRPP, cotisations salariales total, charges patronales total, coût total employeur, mock bulletin de paie.  
**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal-entry records payroll; this *calculates* it. They are designed to be used in sequence — compute the bulletin here, then record the entry there.
