### Accessibility

#### LOW — Checkbox in FeedbackForm has no programmatic label

**Severity: low** | `src/lib/components/feedback/FeedbackForm.svelte:208–210`

```svelte
<label class="context-toggle">
  <input type="checkbox" bind:checked={includeContext} />
  <span>{$t('feedback.context.include')}</span>
</label>
```

The checkbox is inside a `<label>` (so implicit association exists), but wrapping-label association does not work with all screen readers when the label contains child elements. Adding an explicit `id` + `for` pair or an `aria-label` on the input would be more robust.

**Suggested fix:** Add `id="feedback-include-context"` to the `<input>` and `for="feedback-include-context"` to the `<label>`, or add `aria-label={$t('feedback.context.include')}` directly on the `<input>`.

### Playground 1 — Inventory Valuation Methods
**EN:** Inventory Valuation — FIFO, LIFO, WAC  
**FR:** Valorisation des Stocks — FIFO, CMUP, DEPS

**Target learner:** Licence 2, Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** Students routinely confuse how the cost-flow assumption (FIFO vs. weighted-average cost vs. DEPS) affects ending inventory, COGS, and gross profit. SYSCOHADA mandates CMUP or FIFO — the playground reinforces why the choice is not neutral.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a sequence of purchase lots (date, quantity, unit cost) and sales events. Three side-by-side panels show the running stock card under each method. Clicking any row highlights the units consumed and their cost basis. A summary KPI strip shows ending inventory value, COGS, and gross profit delta across methods.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: up to 10 stock movements (type: purchase/sale, qty, unit cost for purchases)
- Outputs: stock card per method, ending inventory, COGS, gross profit, a bar chart comparing the three

**Complements existing:** The journal-entry playground records the accounting entry; this playground teaches *what number goes into* the COGS line — a natural prerequisite.

### Playground 2 — Financial Ratio Analysis
**EN:** Financial Ratio Dashboard  
**FR:** Tableau de Bord des Ratios Financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can memorize ratio formulas but rarely understand how changing one balance-sheet or income-statement item ripples through the entire ratio picture. This playground builds that intuition.

**Core interaction:** Learner fills in a simplified SYSCOHADA-format balance sheet and income statement (pre-populated with a realistic FCFA enterprise scenario). A live dashboard updates 12 ratios (liquidity: current, quick; solvency: debt ratio, interest coverage; activity: inventory turnover, receivables days; profitability: ROA, ROE, net margin, operating margin; OHADA-specific: financial autonomy ratio, cash ratio). Sliders on key line items let learners run "what-if" — drag receivables up, see the quick ratio drop in real time.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: ~20 balance-sheet line items + 8 income-statement line items (SYSCOHADA classes 1–5, 6–7)
- Outputs: 12 ratio cards with colour-coded thresholds (green/amber/red), sparkline showing sensitivity to the most recent change

**Complements existing:** The journal-entry playground records transactions; this playground shows what those transactions do to the ratios that lenders and investors actually read.

### Playground 3 — Payroll & Social Charges Calculator
**EN:** OHADA Payroll & Social Contribution Calculator  
**FR:** Simulateur de Paie et Charges Sociales

**Target learner:** Licence 2, practicing HR/accounting professional

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone payroll involves country-specific social contribution ceilings and rates (CNPS Cameroun, INPS Côte d'Ivoire, etc.) that students rarely internalize. A miscalculated gross-to-net is the #1 error in entry-level accounting jobs in the region.

**Core interaction:** Learner selects country (dropdown: CM, CI, SN, GA, BJ — each with its statutory rates), enters gross salary, transport allowance, and seniority years. The playground computes employee deductions (CNPS old-age, health, work-accident), employer charges, net salary, and the journal entry for the payroll expense (charge 66x / charge 64x split). A toggle switches between monthly and annual view.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: country selector, gross salary, allowances, seniority, number of dependants
- Outputs: net-salary waterfall chart, employer cost total, auto-generated journal entry lines (debit salary expense, credit social contributions payable, credit net salary payable)

**Complements existing:** Produces output that feeds directly into the journal-entry playground — a pedagogical chain from gross pay to the accounting record.

### Playground 4 — Standard Costing & Variance Analysis
**EN:** Standard Costing & Variance Analysis  
**FR:** Coût Standard et Analyse des Écarts

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master (managerial accounting)

**Pedagogical objective:** Variance analysis (price, quantity/efficiency, volume) is taught algorithmically but students rarely see why decomposing a total variance into price and usage components is useful for management decisions. This is a core Master-level managerial topic.

**Core interaction:** Learner sets standard cost per unit (material, labour, overhead) and standard quantities, then enters actual production volume and actual costs. Three panels show: (1) the standard cost card, (2) the actual-cost card, (3) the variance tree — total → material (price + usage) → labour (rate + efficiency) → overhead (volume + expenditure). Clicking any variance node shows the formula being applied, with the learner's numbers substituted in.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: standard qty × rate for material, labour, fixed overhead; actual qty, actual rate, actual production volume, budgeted volume
- Outputs: variance tree (favourable/adverse colour-coded), reconciliation statement from standard profit to actual profit, formula card

**Complements existing:** Pure financial-accounting playgrounds (journal entry, amortization) cover classes 1–5. This is the only managerial accounting proposal and fills a gap at the Master level.

### Playground 5 — VAT / TVA Return Builder
**EN:** VAT Return Builder  
**FR:** Constructeur de Déclaration TVA

**Target learner:** Licence 2, Licence 3, practicing accountant

**Pedagogical objective:** In OHADA-zone countries TVA rates (standard 18–19.25%, reduced, exempt) and the deductibility rules for input tax are a perennial source of errors. Students confuse "TVA collectée" with "TVA déductible" and miss partial-deductibility for mixed-use purchases.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a list of sales transactions (amount HT, TVA rate) and purchase transactions (amount HT, TVA rate, deductibility coefficient 0–1). The playground builds the TVA return form (a simplified Cerfa-style layout), computing TVA collectée, TVA déductible brute, TVA déductible nette (after coefficient), TVA à payer / crédit de TVA. Toggle between OHADA standard and the learner's country rate table. A second tab shows the journal entries (4456 / 4457 / 4458 accounts in SYSCOHADA PCG).

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: up to 15 sales lines (HT amount, rate), up to 15 purchase lines (HT amount, rate, deductibility %), reporting period month
- Outputs: TVA return summary, net TVA payable, auto-generated journal entries using SYSCOHADA account numbers, error alert if deductibility coefficient > 1

**Complements existing:** The chart-of-accounts page already exposes the SYSCOHADA PCG. This playground teaches how to *use* accounts 445x in practice — closing a loop from theory to compliance form.
