### BUG-01 — Emoji icons violate CLAUDE.md "No emoji icons" rule
**Severity:** high  
**File:** `src/lib/data/playgrounds.ts:18,27,36,44,53,61,69`

All 7 playground entries use emoji strings as `icon` values (📐 📊 📒 🏦 📉 🏧 📈). CLAUDE.md explicitly bans emoji icons. These render via `PlaygroundCard.svelte:13` (`{pg.icon}`) and will be sent over the wire as emoji codepoints, which vary visually across platforms and can't be styled via CSS.

**Suggested fix:** Replace each emoji with a compact SVG symbol (inline or via sprite), using `aria-hidden="true"` and sizing via CSS. Tokens already define `--radius-*` and `--accent` that chart icons can use.

### BUG-02 — Hardcoded hex palette in chart components bypasses theme
**Severity:** high  
**Files:**
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/CategoryBreakdownDonut.svelte:31–39`
- `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/MatchingPairsOverlay.svelte:31–34`

Both components define JS color maps (e.g. `'nsf-check': '#ef4444'`, `fuzzy: '#f59e0b'`) that are passed directly to SVG `stroke`/`fill` attributes. These hex values cannot reference CSS custom properties, meaning a theme change in `tokens.ts` won't reach these charts. Nine distinct hard-coded colors in `CategoryBreakdownDonut`, four in `MatchingPairsOverlay`.

**Suggested fix:** Define a CATEGORY_COLORS export in `tokens.ts` (or a separate `src/lib/theme/chart-palette.ts`) and import from there. Where SVG attributes must be hex, compute the palette from tokens once at the module level so a single file controls all chart colors.

### BUG-03 — `color: #fff` used instead of `var(--text-primary)`
**Severity:** medium  
**Files:**
- `src/lib/playgrounds/journal-entry/components/EntryHistory.svelte:400`
- `src/routes/[lang]/chart-of-accounts/+page.svelte:423,458`
- `src/routes/[lang]/learn/+page.svelte:202`

Four CSS declarations use `color: #fff` as a literal white override. This is almost certainly intended as high-contrast text on colored backgrounds (e.g. the error-red confirm button), but it still hardcodes a hex value outside the token system. In a future theme, `--text-primary` may be off-white; the hardcoded `#fff` will diverge.

**Suggested fix:** For buttons with colored backgrounds use `color: var(--text-primary)` which the theme already sets to `#eef0f6`. If pure white is needed for contrast against a saturated background, add a `--text-on-accent` token to `tokens.ts`.

### BUG-07 — Dynamic i18n key defeats `TranslationKey` type guard
**Severity:** low  
**File:** `src/routes/[lang]/+page.svelte:47–48`

```svelte
$t(`features.${feat.key}.title`)
$t(`features.${feat.key}.desc`)
```

`feat.key` is a `const` union inferred as `'interactive' | 'bilingual' | 'syscohada' | 'free'`, so the keys are actually safe at runtime. However, TypeScript resolves the template literal as `string`, bypassing the `TranslationKey` union. This means a future rename of the feature keys in the namespace files won't produce a compile error here.

**Suggested fix:** Extract the full translated strings into the `features` array directly:
```ts
const features = [
  { titleKey: 'features.interactive.title' as TranslationKey, ... },
] as const;
```
Or cast: `` $t(`features.${feat.key}.title` as TranslationKey) ``.

### PG-01 — Inventory Valuation Methods
**EN:** Inventory Valuation Methods | **FR:** Méthodes d'évaluation des stocks

**Target learner:** Licence 2, Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** Students consistently conflate FIFO, LIFO, and Weighted Average Cost (CMUP), and misapply SYSCOHADA's preference for CMUP. The playground makes the impact of method choice on COGS and closing stock concrete and immediate.

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs opening stock (qty + unit cost), then a chronological series of purchase and sale transactions. Three side-by-side columns show inventory ledgers calculated under FIFO, CMUP (perpetual and periodic variants), and for comparison LIFO. KPIs update live: COGS, gross margin, closing stock value. A toggle forces the learner to reconcile which method SYSCOHADA Révisé mandates for each asset category.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: opening stock, up to 10 purchase/sale lines (date, qty, unit price)
- Outputs: movement schedule per method, ending inventory value, COGS, gross margin delta vs CMUP

**Complements existing playgrounds:** Depreciation covers non-current asset cost allocation; this covers current asset cost flow. Journal Entry playground can reference COGS and inventory accounts generated here.

### PG-02 — Receivables Aging & Doubtful Debt Provision
**EN:** Receivables Aging & Provision | **FR:** Analyse des créances et provision pour dépréciation

**Target learner:** Licence 3, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Many OHADA-zone finance students do not distinguish between writing off a bad debt and recognizing a provision. The aging grid mechanic makes the probability-of-loss concept tangible and reinforces the SYSCOHADA rule that provisions flow through Résultat, not equity.

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs a receivables ledger (customer, invoice date, amount). The playground auto-buckets invoices into aging brackets (current, 30–60d, 60–90d, 90–180d, >180d). Learner assigns (or accepts default SYSCOHADA) provision rates per bracket. The playground computes the required provision balance, the adjustment entry vs. prior-year provision, and generates the adjusting journal entry text ready to paste into the Journal Entry playground.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: up to 20 receivable lines, provision rate per aging bracket
- Outputs: aging schedule, provision computation, net realizable value, adjusting journal entry

**Complements existing playgrounds:** Bank Reconciliation identifies cash timing differences; this covers the credit-risk side of current assets.

### PG-03 — Payroll & Employer Social Charges
**EN:** Payroll & Social Charges | **FR:** Paie et charges sociales patronales

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Payroll accounting is routinely described as the most error-prone area for junior accountants in OHADA businesses. Gross-to-net, CNSS contributions, and IRPP withholding rates differ by country within the OHADA zone. Students lack a tool that shows the full payslip-to-journal-entry chain.

**Core interaction:** Learner selects OHADA country (Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal, etc.), enters employee salary, allowances, and family situation. The playground derives: CNSS employee + employer shares, IRPP withholding bracket computation, net salary, total employer cost. Outputs: a formatted payslip breakdown, the full set of payroll journal entries (Account 66 — Charges de personnel), and a net salary check figure.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: country, gross salary, transport allowance, dependants
- Outputs: CNSS base, IRPP taxable income, withholding amount, net pay, employer total cost, journal entries

**Complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry playground handles generic entries; this generates the specific payroll entry set automatically.

### PG-04 — Financial Ratio Dashboard
**EN:** Financial Ratio Dashboard | **FR:** Tableau de bord des ratios financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master

**Pedagogical objective:** Students who can prepare a balance sheet often cannot interpret it. The misconception this fixes: a high net profit does not mean the business is solvent or liquid. Interactive sliders make the tension between profitability, liquidity, and solvency visceral.

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs a simplified SYSCOHADA balance sheet (Actif immobilisé, Actif circulant, Trésorerie; Capitaux propres, Dettes financières, Dettes circulantes) and income statement (CA, charges d'exploitation, résultat). The playground computes a full ratio suite: autonomie financière, liquidité générale/réduite/immédiate, rentabilité économique/financière (ROA/ROE), ratio d'endettement, BFR, trésorerie nette. A color-coded gauge for each ratio shows position vs. SYSCOHADA/OHADA sector benchmarks.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: ~12 balance sheet line items, 5 income statement line items
- Outputs: 12+ ratios with gauges, narrative "diagnosis" using i18n text

**Complements existing playgrounds:** CVP measures internal profitability levers; this synthesizes external financial health from published accounts.

### PG-05 — VAT / TVA Reconciliation
**EN:** VAT Reconciliation | **FR:** Réconciliation TVA (déclaration de TVA)

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** TVA monthly declarations are among the most common sources of penalties for SMEs in the OHADA zone. Students do not understand the offsetting mechanism (TVA collectée − TVA déductible = TVA à payer/crédit). The playground builds the mental model through a transaction-by-transaction approach.

**Core interaction:** Learner posts a series of monthly transactions (sales with TVA collectée, eligible purchases with TVA déductible, ineligible purchases) with country-selectable rates (Cameroon 19.25%, Côte d'Ivoire 18%, Sénégal 18%, etc.). The playground builds the TVA account (Account 4431/4432) in real time, computes net TVA due or credit carried forward, and generates the declaration summary and the settlement journal entry.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: country (selects rate), up to 15 transaction lines (type, HT amount, TVA rate)
- Outputs: TVA collected, TVA deductible, net position, credit c/f, payment journal entry

**Complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry handles mechanics; this adds the declaratory/fiscal layer that connects accounting to tax compliance.
