### 1.1 Hardcoded hex color palette in donut chart — HIGH
**File:** `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/CategoryBreakdownDonut.svelte:30–39`

`CATEGORY_COLORS` maps 9 `ItemCategory` values to raw hex literals (`#6ea8fe`, `#22c55e`, `#f59e0b`, `#10b981`, `#ef4444`, `#a855f7`, `#06b6d4`, `#ec4899`, `#fb923c`). These are set as SVG `stroke` attributes and as the `background` of legend swatches via an inline `style` binding.

**Why it matters:** The theme system in `tokens.ts` is the single source of truth for color. These 9 values bypass it entirely: they will not adapt if the palette is updated, they carry no semantic meaning, and several (`#6ea8fe` blue, `#a855f7` purple) are not harmonically derived from the accent scale. The legend swatch also uses an inline `style="background: {seg.color}"` which violates the "No inline styles" rule.

**Suggested fix:** Add 9 semantic category tokens to `tokens.ts` (e.g., `catOutstandingCheck`, `catDepositInTransit`, …), expose them as `--cat-*` CSS custom properties via `css-generator.ts`, and reference them via `stroke="var(--cat-outstanding-check)"` in SVG and a CSS class per category for the legend swatch. Eliminate the inline style.

### 1.6 Incomplete WAI-ARIA tabs pattern — MEDIUM
**File:** `src/lib/components/playground/PlaygroundTabs.svelte` and `src/lib/components/playground/PlaygroundShell.svelte`

Tab buttons carry `role="tab"` and `aria-selected` but lack `aria-controls` pointing to a panel ID. The corresponding content areas have no `role="tabpanel"` or `aria-labelledby`. Per ARIA 1.2, a complete tabs widget requires the full triad: `tablist → tab[aria-controls] → tabpanel[aria-labelledby]`.

Screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver) may announce the tab bar but will not convey the association to the visible panel content.

**Suggested fix:** Assign stable IDs to each tab and panel (e.g., `id="tab-learn"` / `id="panel-learn"`). Add `aria-controls="panel-{key}"` to each `<button role="tab">` and wrap each panel section in `<div role="tabpanel" id="panel-{key}" aria-labelledby="tab-{key}" tabindex="0">`.

### 2.1 Financial Ratio Dashboard
**EN:** Financial Ratio Analysis | **FR:** Analyse des ratios financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Corrects the widespread confusion between solvency and liquidity, and between profitability ratios computed on different bases (net income vs. operating income vs. EBITDA). Students who can prepare a balance sheet often cannot interpret what the numbers mean for a going concern.

**Core interaction:** User inputs a simplified SYSCOHADA bilan (balance sheet) and compte de résultat (income statement) — either via direct numeric fields or by choosing a preset scenario (start-up, mature firm, distressed firm). The dashboard recomputes 12 ratios live across four families: liquidity, solvency, profitability, and activity. A traffic-light indicator flags ratios outside OHADA-zone sector benchmarks. Learners can drag a slider to deteriorate one line item and watch the ripple across all ratios.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Total assets, current assets, cash, inventories, receivables, current liabilities, long-term debt, equity, revenue, COGS, EBIT, net income.

**Minimum viable outputs:** Current ratio, quick ratio, debt-to-equity, interest coverage, ROA, ROE, gross margin, operating margin, net margin, asset turnover, receivables days, inventory days.

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** TVM and Amortization focus on individual transactions; this playground synthesises an entire set of financial statements into diagnostic insight — bridging individual mechanics to business analysis.

### 2.2 Payroll Computation (OHADA Zone)
**EN:** Payroll & Social Charges | **FR:** Bulletin de paie et charges sociales

**Target learner:** Licence 2/3, Master, HR/accounting professional

**Pedagogical objective:** The CNPS contribution schedule and IRF (Impôt sur les Revenus des Personnes Physiques) brackets are opaque to most students who have never prepared a pay slip. Errors in social charge computations are among the most common audit findings in OHADA-zone SMEs.

**Core interaction:** User enters gross salary, employment category (cadre/non-cadre), and country (Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal — each with distinct CNPS rates and tax tables). The playground generates a full bulletin de paie showing: gross salary, CNPS employee contribution, CNPS employer contribution, taxable income, IRF bracket decomposition, net-to-pay, and total employment cost. A toggle compares the full-time vs. part-time equivalent.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Gross monthly salary, country selector, employment category, number of dependants (for tax allowances).

**Minimum viable outputs:** Bulletin de paie layout (itemised deductions), employer total cost, effective tax rate, CNPS breakdown, net salary.

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground touches labour accounting. Payroll is a core competency for Licence 2 students and every practicing accountant in the region.

### 2.3 Inventory Valuation (FIFO / CMUP)
**EN:** Inventory Valuation Methods | **FR:** Méthodes d'évaluation des stocks

**Target learner:** Licence 1/2

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA mandates Coût Moyen Unitaire Pondéré (CMUP) as the default inventory costing method, yet most students cannot explain why FIFO and CMUP diverge during inflation, or how the choice affects COGS, closing stock value, and net income. The confusion is compounded because IFRS permits FIFO but not LIFO, while SYSCOHADA diverges on timing.

**Core interaction:** User builds a sequence of stock movements (receipts and issues) by entering date, quantity, and unit cost for each line. The playground simultaneously shows the FIFO fiche de stock and the CMUP fiche de stock side by side, with a summary table comparing: closing stock quantity, closing stock value, COGS for the period, and reported gross profit given a fixed selling price. A difference-highlighting layer makes divergence visible during inflationary sequences.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Up to 12 stock movement lines (type: receipt/issue, quantity, unit cost). Selling price per unit (for gross profit computation).

**Minimum viable outputs:** FIFO fiche de stock, CMUP fiche de stock, comparative summary (closing value, COGS, gross profit), method-difference delta.

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry playground handles the bookkeeping side; this playground handles the valuation decision that feeds into those entries — a natural prerequisite.

### 2.4 Working Capital & Cash Conversion Cycle
**EN:** Working Capital Analysis | **FR:** Fonds de roulement et BFR

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master

**Pedagogical objective:** Students frequently confuse Fonds de Roulement (FR), Besoin en Fonds de Roulement (BFR), and Trésorerie Nette (TN), and cannot diagnose whether a funding gap is structural or cyclical. The relationship FR = BFR + TN is memorised but not understood.

**Core interaction:** User fills in a simplified SYSCOHADA bilan (permanent capital, fixed assets, current assets by nature, current liabilities by nature). The playground renders the three-box cash flow structure diagram (FR / BFR / TN) with annotated arrows. Sliders allow the user to simulate: extending customer payment terms, negotiating supplier terms, or growing revenue — and observe how each move tightens or widens the cash gap. A colour-coded zone indicator flags when TN turns negative.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Fixed assets, equity + long-term debt (permanent capital), trade receivables, inventories, other current assets, trade payables, other current liabilities. Revenue growth rate slider (for projection mode).

**Minimum viable outputs:** FR, BFR, TN (absolute values and as days of revenue), three-zone diagram, cash gap alert, scenario comparison table.

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Amortization and TVM cover individual financing decisions; this playground zooms out to operating cycle dynamics — a concept at the intersection of financial accounting and corporate finance that every Licence 3 student needs before they touch DCF.

### 2.5 VAT Computation & Declaration
**EN:** VAT Return | **FR:** Déclaration de TVA

**Target learner:** Licence 3, Master, tax/accounting professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Distinguishing collectible VAT from deductible input VAT, computing the net balance, and knowing when a VAT credit can be carried forward or refunded are core professional skills rarely taught interactively. Errors in this area carry direct fiscal penalty risk.

**Core interaction:** User enters a list of sales transactions (HT amount, VAT rate, taxable/exempt classification) and a list of purchase transactions (HT amount, VAT rate, eligible for deduction yes/no). The playground computes TVA collectée, TVA déductible, and TVA à décaisser / crédit de TVA. A VAT return form layout (matching the Cameroon DGI DECLAR form structure, as the largest OHADA-zone economy) auto-fills from the computed totals. A toggle switches between monthly and quarterly filing periods.

**Minimum viable inputs:** Up to 20 sales lines (HT, rate: 0% / 19.25% for Cameroon / selectable), up to 20 purchase lines (HT, rate, deductible toggle). Filing period selector.

**Minimum viable outputs:** TVA collectée subtotal, TVA déductible subtotal, net balance, carry-forward vs. payment decision, filled declaration form layout.

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry covers double-entry mechanics; VAT Declaration covers the regulatory reporting layer on top — completing the "transaction to tax return" workflow that every practising accountant must navigate.
