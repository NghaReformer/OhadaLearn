## 1. Bugs & Issues

### [HIGH] Undefined CSS custom properties `--orange`, `--blue`, `--red` with hardcoded hex fallbacks

`css-generator.ts` emits these vars from `tokens.ts`:
`--green`, `--amber`, `--error`, `--accent` (and their derivatives).

It does **not** emit `--orange`, `--blue`, or `--red`. Every reference to those names silently falls through to the hardcoded hex fallback — making them functionally identical to hardcoded colors, in violation of the CLAUDE.md token rule.

| Severity | File | Lines | Value |
|----------|------|-------|-------|
| high | `src/lib/components/playground/KpiStrip.svelte` | 76 | `var(--orange, #f59e0b)` |
| high | `src/lib/playgrounds/amortization/components/ScheduleTable.svelte` | 240, 246, 259, 265 | `var(--orange/--blue/--red/--green, #hex)` |
| high | `src/lib/playgrounds/amortization/components/ChartPanel.svelte` | 436, 444, 448, 493, 501, 505, 515, 529, 533 | `var(--orange/--blue/--green, #hex)` |
| high | `src/lib/playgrounds/amortization/components/InputsPanel.svelte` | 604–605 | `var(--red, #ef4444)` |
| high | `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/LedgerPanel.svelte` | 175 | `var(--orange, #f59e0b)` |

**Suggested fix:**  
- Replace `var(--orange, …)` → `var(--amber)` (token exists).  
- Replace `var(--red, …)` → `var(--error)` (token exists).  
- Replace `var(--blue, …)` → `var(--accent)` (closest token), or add `--blue` to `tokens.ts` + `css-generator.ts` if a distinct brand blue is needed.  
- Remove hex fallbacks once the correct token names are in use.  
- Note: `var(--green, …)`, `var(--error, …)`, `var(--accent, …)` used elsewhere are fine — those vars **are** emitted and the hex fallback is redundant but harmless.

### A. Trial Balance → Financial Statements Mapper
**EN:** Trial Balance to Financial Statements  
**FR:** Balance de Vérification vers États Financiers  
**Target learner:** Licence 2–3

**Pedagogical objective:** Students consistently confuse which SYSCOHADA account classes belong to the Bilan versus the Compte de Résultat, and which balances flip sign. This playground fixes that mapping step — the most error-prone part of the closing cycle.

**Core interaction:** Learner is given a pre-populated trial balance (20–30 accounts). They classify each line by dragging it into the correct financial statement section (non-current assets / current assets / equity / liabilities / revenue / expense). Instant feedback per placement; final view renders a two-column Bilan and a vertical Compte de Résultat.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Input: editable trial balance debit/credit columns (seeded from a scenario)  
- Output: rendered Bilan + Compte de Résultat, résultat net computed and validated

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry teaches *recording*; this playground teaches the *closing step* that connects the ledger to published financials — the missing link in the current curriculum arc.

### B. TVA / VAT Lifecycle Calculator
**EN:** VAT Lifecycle — Input Tax, Output Tax, Declaration  
**FR:** Cycle TVA — TVA Collectée, Déductible, Déclaration  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** VAT mechanics are abstract until students see that a business simultaneously holds a liability (TVA collectée) and a recoverable claim (TVA déductible), and that only the net flows to the tax authority. The SYSCOHADA journal entries for Classes 4/6/7 are rarely taught interactively.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a set of purchase and sale invoices for a month. The playground computes TVA collectée, TVA déductible, net TVA payable/receivable, and generates the complete journal entries (using the COA picker already built). A "declare" button produces a summary declaration table.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Input: list of invoices (HT amount, TVA rate, buy/sell toggle)  
- Output: TVA account reconciliation, journal entries, monthly declaration summary

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground touches indirect taxation. It reuses `AccountPicker`, `NumberField`, and the journal entry rendering infrastructure directly.

### C. Financial Ratio Dashboard
**EN:** Financial Ratio Analysis  
**FR:** Tableau de Bord des Ratios Financiers  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can compute individual ratios but struggle to *read* a ratio suite as a coherent story about a firm. This playground foregrounds interpretation over calculation — the key skill at Master level.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters key line items from a simplified Bilan + Compte de Résultat (or picks a preset scenario representing a healthy, distressed, or growing firm). The playground computes ~12 SYSCOHADA-relevant ratios grouped into: solvabilité, liquidité, rentabilité, gestion (fonds de roulement net, BFR, trésorerie nette). Each ratio has a reference band; a traffic-light strip highlights anomalies. A "compare sector" overlay positions the firm against a sector benchmark.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Input: ~15 financial line items (or scenario selector)  
- Output: ratio grid with benchmark bands, narrative interpretation text (i18n)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP addresses internal break-even; this covers external financial health assessment — a different lens. The `KpiStrip` component is the seed for the ratio grid.

### D. Foreign Currency Transaction & Revaluation
**EN:** Foreign Currency Transactions  
**FR:** Opérations en Devises Étrangères  
**Target learner:** Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** Students do not understand that a foreign-currency receivable/payable has *two* recognition moments (initial and revaluation) producing unrealized exchange gains/losses. This is a common SYSCOHADA exam topic with a specific two-entry pattern.

**Core interaction:** Learner records a sale or purchase invoice in a foreign currency (EUR, USD, GBP — reusing the 16-currency formatter already in `src/lib/format/`). At period end, they enter the closing exchange rate. The playground shows the initial entry at historic rate, the revaluation entry (gain or loss to Class 4), and the settlement entry. A timeline graphic shows the rate movement and resulting P&L impact.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Input: invoice amount + original rate + closing rate + settlement rate  
- Output: three journal entries, cumulative FX gain/loss, worked explanation per step

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry teaches domestic transactions only. This adds the currency dimension without duplicating anything. The `format/` currency system and `AccountPicker` are both reusable directly.

### E. SYSCOHADA Payroll Builder
**EN:** Payroll Calculation & Journal Entry  
**FR:** Calcul de la Paie et Écriture de Salaire  
**Target learner:** Licence 2–3

**Pedagogical objective:** The payroll cycle (brut → cotisations salariales CNSS → net à payer → charges patronales → écritures de salaire) is among the most-feared practical topics in OHADA-zone accounting programs because it involves simultaneously tracking four Classes (4, 6, 4, 5). No existing playground touches it.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters gross salary and selects the OHADA country (Cameroun, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal — different CNSS rate tables). The playground computes salary net of employee deductions, employer social charges, net payable, and generates the two canonical journal entries (bulletin de paie entry + payment entry) in SYSCOHADA format.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**  
- Input: gross salary, contract type, country selector (rate table)  
- Output: pay-slip summary table, two journal entries, breakdown of each deduction line

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Covers the full HR-to-accounting pipeline, which is absent from current offerings. Uses `AccountPicker`, `NumberField`, and the existing scenario architecture. Country-specific rate tables make it immediately relevant across the OHADA zone.
