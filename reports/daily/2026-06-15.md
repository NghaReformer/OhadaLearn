### P1 — Inventory Valuation Methods
**EN:** Inventory Valuation Explorer | **FR:** Explorateur de méthodes d'évaluation des stocks

**Target learner:** Licence 2–3 (financial accounting year)

**Pedagogical objective:** Students consistently confuse FIFO, LIFO (allowed in OHADA), and Weighted-Average Cost (CMUP) outcomes and cannot explain why the same physical stock produces different profit figures under each method — a classic SYSCOHADA exam trap.

**Core interaction:** Learner enters a sequence of purchase lots (date, quantity, unit cost) and sales events, then switches between FIFO / LIFO / CMUP tabs to see how stock value, COGS, and gross profit change. A divergence strip (like the interest playground's `DivergenceRibbon`) highlights the gap between methods.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: up to 10 purchase rows (qty, unit cost), up to 5 sales (qty), method selector
- Outputs: stock card (SYSCOHADA account 31x), COGS entry, ending inventory value, gross profit per method, comparison table

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal entry teaches *how* to post; this teaches *what* to post for stock transactions and *why* the choice of method matters. It would feed naturally into the journal-entry playground as a scenario source.

### P2 — Financial Ratio Dashboard
**EN:** Financial Ratio Analyzer | **FR:** Analyseur de ratios financiers

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1 (financial analysis module)

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can compute isolated ratios but struggle to read a ratio set holistically and spot which ratios signal sector-specific stress in OHADA-zone enterprises (high leverage is normal in infrastructure; low liquidity is not in retail).

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs a simplified balance sheet and income statement (pre-filled from a scenario: retail SME, manufacturing firm, service company). The playground computes the six canonical SYSCOHADA ratios (autonomie financière, solvabilité, liquidité générale, rentabilité nette, rotation des stocks, délai clients) and renders a spider/radar chart. Learner drills into any ratio to see the formula and which SYSCOHADA account lines feed it.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: 8–12 balance sheet line items + 4 income statement items, scenario picker
- Outputs: radar chart with 6 ratios, individual ratio cards with formula reveal, benchmark band (sector average)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP covers operational profitability in isolation. This ties balance-sheet health to operations and teaches the *full picture* analysts use — a gap no existing playground fills.

### P3 — VAT / TVA Compliance Calculator
**EN:** VAT Compliance Simulator | **FR:** Simulateur de conformité TVA

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / practicing professional (tax module)

**Pedagogical objective:** TVA declarations in the OHADA zone are error-prone because rates vary by country and product category (e.g., Cameroon: 19.25% standard, 0% exports; Ivory Coast: 18%; Senegal: 18%), and students confuse TVA collectée, TVA déductible, and TVA à décaisser/reverser.

**Core interaction:** Learner selects a country, enters a month's worth of purchase invoices (with TVA) and sales invoices (with TVA), and the playground builds the monthly TVA declaration: collectée − déductible = net due / credit carry-forward. A toggle shows the journal entries (accounts 4431, 4452, 4455 per SYSCOHADA plan).

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: country selector (rate auto-populated), up to 10 purchase rows, up to 10 sale rows, reverse-charge toggle
- Outputs: TVA declaration summary, net payable, journal entry preview (linkable to journal-entry playground)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground touches tax compliance; this is one of the highest-stakes real-world tasks for OHADA practitioners and a frequent exam topic.

### P4 — Budget vs. Actual Variance Analysis
**EN:** Budget Variance Analyzer | **FR:** Analyseur d'écarts budgétaires

**Target learner:** Master 1–2 (management accounting / contrôle de gestion)

**Pedagogical objective:** Students often compute variances correctly but misinterpret sign conventions (favorable vs. unfavorable) and cannot decompose a cost variance into price effect and volume effect — the two-part variance analysis required in SYSCOHADA management-accounting exams.

**Core interaction:** Learner inputs budgeted and actual figures for revenue, direct material, direct labor, and fixed overhead. The playground decomposes each variance (écart global → écart sur prix + écart sur quantité for materials; écart sur taux + écart sur temps for labor). Color-coded bars show F/U direction. A "drill-down" toggle expands the formula derivation.

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: budget vs. actual for 4 cost/revenue categories, standard quantities and rates
- Outputs: global variance table, decomposed sub-variances, F/U badges, management commentary template (EN/FR)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP operates at break-even level; this playground operates at the *control* level — comparing plan to reality, the next step in the management accounting curriculum.

### P5 — Payroll & Social Charges Calculator
**EN:** Payroll & Social Charges Simulator | **FR:** Simulateur de paie et charges sociales

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / practicing professional (social law / HR accounting module)

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone payroll involves layered contributions (CNPS, CRTV levy, professional tax, housing fund) with country-specific rates and salary tranches. Students — and many practitioners — cannot reconcile gross-to-net and correctly split employer vs. employee charges.

**Core interaction:** Learner selects a country (Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal), enters gross salary, transport allowance, and housing allowance. The playground computes net salary, itemised employee deductions, and employer contributions, then generates the payroll journal entries (accounts 641, 421, 431, 447 per SYSCOHADA).

**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:**
- Inputs: country, gross salary, allowances, number of dependants (for tax relief)
- Outputs: pay slip breakdown, employer cost summary, SYSCOHADA journal entries for the month, annual projection

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground covers social accounting or payroll, yet it is mandatory in every Licence 3 accounting curriculum across the OHADA zone and one of the most request-heavy topics in professional training.
