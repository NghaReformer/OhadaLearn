### PG-1 — Closing Entries Simulator  
**EN:** Closing Entries Simulator · **FR:** Simulateur d'Écritures de Clôture

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master 1

**Pedagogical objective:** Students consistently confuse the SYSCOHADA year-end sequence: adjusting entries (charges constatées d'avance, produits à recevoir) → closing expense/revenue accounts to "Résultat de l'Exercice" (class 12) → appropriating profit. This simulator forces them to execute each step in order and see the balance sheet update in real time.

**Core interaction:** A pre-loaded trial balance (editable). Student drags or assigns accounts to the correct closing step, enters the journal entry, and sees the updated post-closing trial balance + bilan skeleton populate live. Errors unlock a step-by-step hint sequence.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: trial balance rows (account code, debit/credit balance), exercise date
- Outputs: adjusting-entries journal, income summary journal, post-closing trial balance, balance carried to "Bilan"

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry covers individual entries; this playground covers the *sequence* across an entire exercise, bridging journal entry mechanics to financial statement preparation.

### PG-2 — SYSCOHADA Financial Ratios Analyzer  
**EN:** Financial Ratio Analyzer · **FR:** Analyseur de Ratios Financiers SYSCOHADA

**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA financial ratios use different aggregates than IFRS/US-GAAP (e.g., Fonds de Roulement Net Global, Besoin en Fonds de Roulement, Trésorerie Nette). Students confuse these OHADA-specific concepts with generic liquidity ratios.

**Core interaction:** Two input panels — SYSCOHADA Bilan (assets/liabilities in OHADA class structure) and Compte de Résultat — pre-populated with a case scenario. Student adjusts line items; a live dashboard renders FDRNG, BFR, TN, ratios de rentabilité, and autonomie financière with color-coded benchmarks and plain-language interpretation per ratio.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: OHADA bilan line items (classes 1–5 aggregates), turnover, EBITDA
- Outputs: 8–10 ratios with value, benchmark band, one-line interpretation (bilingual)

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Interest and TVM cover isolated financial math; this covers integrated financial analysis, moving learners toward real-world decision-making.

### PG-3 — VAT / TVA Ledger  
**EN:** VAT Ledger Simulator · **FR:** Simulateur de Déclaration de TVA

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** TVA collectée vs. TVA déductible vs. TVA à décaisser / crédit de TVA is one of the highest-failure-rate topics in SYSCOHADA exams, partly because the OHADA-zone rates (18% standard in most UEMOA/CEMAC countries, 0% exports, exempt categories) differ from European examples students read.

**Core interaction:** Student builds a month's transaction list — sales invoices, purchase invoices, mixed (exempt + taxable), imports. The playground auto-posts TVA accounts (4451, 4452, 4455 in SYSCOHADA), calculates the net TVA position, and generates the journal entries for the TVA declaration. Toggle between UEMOA (18%) and CEMAC rates.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: transaction rows (type: sale/purchase/import/exempt, HT amount, applicable rate)
- Outputs: TVA collectée total, TVA déductible total, net position, journal entries, declaration summary

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry handles generic debits/credits; this is domain-specific to indirect tax — a SYSCOHADA exam pillar with no existing coverage.

### PG-4 — Inventory Valuation Methods  
**EN:** Inventory Valuation Methods · **FR:** Méthodes de Valorisation des Stocks

**Target learner:** Licence 2 / Licence 3

**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA allows CMUP (weighted average) and PEPS/FIFO but not LIFO; students frequently apply the wrong method or calculate CMUP incorrectly (perpetual vs. periodic). The playground makes the mechanical difference between methods visceral.

**Core interaction:** Student enters a sequence of stock movements (initial stock, purchases with unit cost, sales). The playground renders three parallel columns — CMUP perpetuel, CMUP périodique, PEPS — showing the running stock ledger, cost of goods sold, and ending inventory value for each method. Sliders let them adjust purchase prices to see how volatility affects each method differently.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: opening stock (qty, unit cost), sequence of receipts (qty, unit price) and issues (qty)
- Outputs: stock ledger per method, COGS per method, closing stock value, variance table

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Amortization and Interest cover financial math; inventory is a core SYSCOHADA *operational* accounting topic untouched by current playgrounds.

### PG-5 — Payroll and Social Charges Calculator  
**EN:** Payroll & Social Charges Calculator · **FR:** Simulateur de Bulletin de Paie et Charges Sociales

**Target learner:** Master 1 / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** OHADA-zone payroll involves CNSS (or CNAMGS/ONIAM depending on country), IRPP/IGR income tax, taxable vs. exempt allowances, and the employer charge rate — all of which vary by country within the zone. Students rarely see a worked example that covers both the employee net pay and the employer's total labour cost in one view.

**Core interaction:** Input gross salary, selected OHADA country (Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroun, Sénégal, Gabon), allowances, and overtime. The playground renders the full payslip (cotisations salariales, IRPP tranche, net à payer) and the employer's cost breakdown (cotisations patronales, charge totale), with journal entries for the payroll provision and payment. Country rates update automatically from a configuration table.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: gross salary, country selector, allowance items (transport, housing — taxable flags), overtime hours
- Outputs: employee payslip breakdown, employer cost summary, payroll journal entries (6611/4311/4321 accounts), net pay

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** No existing playground covers payroll, yet it accounts for a large share of SYSCOHADA class-6 entries in practice. It also exercises the i18n + format systems (currency per country, locale-aware numbers) already built into the platform.
