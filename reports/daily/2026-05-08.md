### BUG-04 · MEDIUM · Playground-page detection regex misses trailing slash

**File:** `src/routes/[lang]/+layout.svelte:13-15`

```ts
let isPlaygroundPage = $derived(
    !!page.url.pathname.match(/^\/[a-z]{2}\/playgrounds\/[^/]+$/)
);
```

A canonical URL with a trailing slash — e.g. `/en/playgrounds/tvm/` — does not match. SvelteKit's default redirect behaviour and some reverse-proxy configurations append trailing slashes. When the regex fails, `Nav` and `Footer` render on playground pages, shrinking the iframe viewport unexpectedly.

**Fix:**

```ts
/^\/[a-z]{2}\/playgrounds\/[^/?#]+\/?$/
```

### BUG-08 · LOW · `step` attribute silently ignored on `type="text"` input

**File:** `src/lib/components/playground/NumberField.svelte:59`

```html
<input type="text" inputmode="decimal" … {step} … />
```

The `step` attribute is only meaningful on `type="number"`, `type="range"`, and date/time inputs. On `type="text"` it is silently discarded by the browser. The prop type is `number | 'any'` so callers pass it expecting validation behaviour that never runs.

**Fix:** Either remove the `step` prop and document that callers should use `min`/`max`/`onChange` for clamping, or switch to programmatic validation in `handleInput` using the `step` value.

### IDEA-01 · Accounts Receivable Ageing

**Title (EN):** Accounts Receivable Ageing Analysis  
**Title (FR):** Analyse des créances par antériorité  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / practicing professional  
**Pedagogical objective:** Students conflate total receivables with collectibility. This playground makes ageing buckets (0–30 d, 31–60 d, 61–90 d, >90 d) tactile and shows how the doubtful-debt provision changes with collection assumptions — a core SYSCOHADA balance-sheet adjustment that is almost always computed mechanically without understanding.  
**Core interaction:** Learner inputs a list of customer invoice lines (amount + age in days). The playground computes the ageing schedule, applies user-adjustable provision rates per bucket, and shows the net realisable value and the journal entry to record the provision (compte 491 in SYSCOHADA).  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Invoice list (amount, days outstanding), provision % per bucket → ageing grid, provision amount, debit 6591 / credit 491 journal entry, donut chart of bucket distribution.  
**Complement:** Bank Reconciliation covers timing differences; this covers credit risk. Journal Entry covers the posting mechanic; this provides a real-world scenario where the amounts are derived, not given.

### IDEA-02 · Statement of Cash Flows Builder (Indirect Method)

**Title (EN):** Cash Flow Statement — Indirect Method  
**Title (FR):** Tableau des flux de trésorerie — méthode indirecte  
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master  
**Pedagogical objective:** The indirect method is the most common in OHADA annual reports but is counterintuitive: net profit is not the same as operating cash. Students routinely misplace depreciation (a non-cash add-back), working capital movements, and investing outflows. No existing playground addresses cash-flow classification.  
**Core interaction:** Learner is given a simplified income statement and two-year balance sheet. They drag-and-drop line items into Operating / Investing / Financing sections. The playground validates the classification and shows the running total; the final "check" computes the change in cash and compares it with the balance sheet difference.  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Pre-filled P&L + BS values (editable), classification dropzone for each item → three-section cash flow statement, net change in cash, pass/fail per line with explanation.  
**Complement:** Journal Entry teaches individual postings; this teaches the aggregate view. TVM teaches the value of future cash; this teaches the origin of current cash.

### IDEA-03 · Working Capital & Current Ratio Simulator

**Title (FR):** Besoin en fonds de roulement et ratios de liquidité

**Pedagogical objective:** BFR (besoin en fonds de roulement) is a foundational SYSCOHADA concept that students learn as a formula but rarely grasp dynamically. ### IDEA-04 · Perpetual Inventory — Weighted Average vs FIFO

**Title (EN):** Inventory Costing: WAC vs FIFO  
**Title (FR):** Coût des stocks : CMUP vs FIFO  
**Target learner:** Licence 2  
**Pedagogical objective:** SYSCOHADA allows Weighted Average Cost (CMUP périodique and CMUP après chaque entrée) and FIFO; students frequently confuse the two methods, get different closing stock values, and cannot explain why gross profit differs. This is a high-frequency exam topic where mechanical errors are common.  
**Core interaction:** Learner inputs a sequence of purchases (date, qty, unit cost) and sales (date, qty). The playground computes the cost of goods sold and ending inventory under both methods simultaneously, with a step-by-step ledger table showing each movement and its cost allocation.  
**Minimum viable inputs/outputs:** Up to 10 purchase/sale rows → side-by-side perpetual ledger (WAC / FIFO), COGS for each method, ending inventory value, gross profit comparison bar chart.
