## 1. Bugs & Issues

### HIGH

#### 1.1 Hardcoded hex colors in `CategoryBreakdownDonut.svelte`
**File:** `src/lib/playgrounds/bank-reconciliation/components/CategoryBreakdownDonut.svelte:31–39`
**Severity:** High

Nine hex literals are defined in a `CATEGORY_COLORS` constant and injected into the DOM
via `style="background: {seg.color}"` (line 120) and as an SVG `stroke` attribute (line 94).
This bypasses the design system entirely — if the theme palette changes, the donut chart
will silently diverge.

```ts
const CATEGORY_COLORS: Record<ItemCategory, string> = {
  'outstanding-check': '#6ea8fe',   // line 31
  'deposit-in-transit': '#22c55e',  // line 32
  'bank-charge': '#f59e0b',         // line 33
  ...
};
```

**Suggested fix:** Add nine `--chart-cat-*` tokens to `src/lib/theme/tokens.ts`
(e.g., `chartCatOutstandingCheck: '#6ea8fe'`), generate CSS vars via the existing
`css-generator.ts` pipeline, and replace the hex literals with `var(--chart-cat-*)` strings.
The `style` attribute usage is acceptable for dynamic SVG stroke/fill as long as the value
itself is a `var()`.

#### 1.3 Unknown `--orange` token with hardcoded hex fallback in `KpiStrip.svelte`
**File:** `src/lib/components/playground/KpiStrip.svelte:76`
**Severity:** Medium

```css
color: var(--orange, #f59e0b);
```

`--orange` is not declared in `src/lib/theme/tokens.ts`. The theme defines `--amber`
(`#f5a623`) which covers the same colour family. Because the custom property is never set,
the browser always falls through to the hardcoded `#f59e0b` hex fallback, making the
design-system layer a no-op for this rule.

**Suggested fix:** Replace `var(--orange, #f59e0b)` with `var(--amber)`. If a distinct
orange token is genuinely needed, add `orange: '#f59e0b'` to `tokens.ts` and remove the
inline fallback.

### 3.1 VAT / TVA Calculator
**EN:** VAT Drill | **FR:** Simulateur de TVA
**Target learner:** Licence 2–3

**Pedagogical objective:** Students routinely confuse TVA collectée (output tax) with TVA
déductible (input tax) and struggle to determine whether a period ends with a balance
payable or a credit carry-forward. OHADA-zone rates vary (17.5 % in most UEMOA states,
19.25 % in Cameroon, 20 % in Morocco) so the playground must expose the rate selector.

**Core interaction:** The learner enters a series of purchases and sales for a period.
The playground computes TVA collectée, TVA déductible, and the net position
(TVA à décaisser or crédit de TVA reporté). A timeline view shows how the credit
carries into the next period.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: HT amount + direction (purchase / sale) per transaction, TVA rate selector
- Outputs: TVA collectée subtotal, TVA déductible subtotal, net balance, journal entries
  for la liquidation

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Journal Entry teaches the mechanics of
double-entry; this playground focuses on the monthly tax cycle that sits on top of it,
which is a distinct cognitive leap most Licence 2 students must make before practicals.

### 3.2 Payroll Builder (Bulletin de Paie SYSCOHADA)
**EN:** Payroll Builder | **FR:** Simulateur de bulletin de paie
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** Payroll computation is multi-step (gross → cotisations sociales
→ retenues IRPP → net) and varies by country/employer size. The biggest misconception is
conflating employer charges with employee deductions. No existing playground covers it.

**Core interaction:** The learner enters a gross salary and selects a country profile
(CNPS-CI, CNSS-CM, IPRES-SN, etc.). The playground calculates and annotates each
deduction column — employee share, employer share — producing a net-to-pay figure and the
two journal entries (paie and charges patronales). Goal-seek can reverse-solve from
desired net to required gross.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: gross salary, country profile, allowances (transport, logement, indemnité)
- Outputs: detailed bulletin strip, employer cost summary, journal entry draft

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Directly extends the Journal Entry playground
domain into payroll, a heavy topic in Licence 3 courses and professional exams.

### 3.3 Inventory Valuation (FIFO / CUMP / LIFO)
**EN:** Inventory Valuation | **FR:** Évaluation des stocks
**Target learner:** Licence 2

**Pedagogical objective:** The learner learns how the choice of valuation method
(FIFO, CUMP, LIFO — noting LIFO is prohibited under SYSCOHADA but is taught comparatively)
affects COGS and end-of-period profit. Misunderstanding this leads to errors in the
compte de résultat and in tax declarations.

**Core interaction:** A table of mouvements (entrées/sorties) over several months.
The learner selects a method; the playground recalculates unit costs, inventory balance,
and COGS for each period. A delta panel shows the profit impact of switching methods —
directly addressing the "it doesn't matter which method you pick" misconception.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: opening stock, unit cost, quantity per movement; method selector
- Outputs: fiche de stock with running balance, COGS per period, period profit impact

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Amortization and Depreciation both deal with
asset value over time; Inventory Valuation extends the same pattern to current assets,
completing the asset lifecycle view.

### 3.4 Financial Ratio Analyser
**EN:** Financial Ratio Analyser | **FR:** Analyseur de ratios financiers
**Target learner:** Licence 3 / Master

**Pedagogical objective:** Students can extract balance-sheet figures but struggle to
contextualise them. This playground teaches why a ratio matters (what decision it
informs) rather than just how to calculate it.

**Core interaction:** The learner either inputs bilan + compte de résultat figures manually
or selects a pre-loaded OHADA-sector scenario (agriculture, trading, services). The
playground computes a panel of key ratios (liquidité générale, solvabilité, ROE, marge
nette, rotation des stocks, délai de recouvrement), displays traffic-light
benchmarks, and lets the learner drag individual line items to simulate restructuring
decisions.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: bilan condensé (5–8 line items), compte de résultat condensé, sector selector
- Outputs: ratio panel with benchmarks, waterfall chart for ROE decomposition, narrative
  interpretation per ratio

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** CVP analyses profitability at the product
level; this playground brings the full-entity financial health view missing from the
current lineup.

### 3.5 Cash Flow Statement Builder (Tableau de Flux — SYSCOHADA)
**EN:** Cash Flow Builder | **FR:** Constructeur de tableau de flux de trésorerie
**Target learner:** Master / practicing professional

**Pedagogical objective:** The indirect method for computing operating cash flow from net
income is consistently the hardest topic in Licence 3 / Master programmes and professional
exams. Students confuse restatements (adding back depreciation, reversing working capital
changes) with actual cash movements.

**Core interaction:** A list of accounting events (depreciation charge, AR increase, AP
decrease, asset purchase, long-term borrowing). The learner drags each event to the correct
section (operating / investing / financing) and marks the sign (add / subtract). The
playground validates placement, shows the running subtotal per section, and compares
the final free-cash-flow against the opening/closing cash balances from the bilan.
A "show me" mode reveals the SYSCOHADA-format tableau directly.

**Minimum viable inputs / outputs:**
- Inputs: pre-loaded scenario (5–10 events), learner classifications per event
- Outputs: partially-completed SYSCOHADA tableau, error indicators per misclassified item,
  reconciliation to cash balance

**Why it complements existing playgrounds:** Bank Reconciliation works at the transaction
level; this playground operates at the reporting level and introduces the cash flow
statement format required by SYSCOHADA-Révisé for entities above the simplified threshold.
